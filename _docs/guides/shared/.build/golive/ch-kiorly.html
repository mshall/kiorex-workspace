CHAPTER 05

Kiorly sign-in, roles and plans

Everything in this chapter is live on kx-test and needs no gate. It explains how people get into Kiorly, who can do what in the consoles, and how plans and prices are managed. None of it records the Egypt gate.

5.1Two ways to sign in

Every Clinic, Pharmacy and Lab, standalone or connected to Kiorex, is bound to Kiorex Passport automatically and offers both ways in, on one shared sign-in card (ADR-061):

On kx-test the Pharmacy and Lab hosts run in a mode where the card shows the Passport button first; the Clinic keeps its Password tab. Pilot and production default to Passport only. This is configuration, not a fault.

5.2MFA policy: off by default

The Passport MFA policy for pharmacy and lab organisations is OFF. While it is off, an owner or pharmacist reaches privileged areas with a phone code alone; an authenticator is optional. Only Passport ops can turn it on, through a maker-checker page, and only by a human. If you do not want owners without an authenticator, switch it on before real owners arrive. Nothing in this guide switches it on.

Trade-off accepted by the founder
With the policy OFF, a stolen phone code is enough to open an owner session. Decide before the first real owner signs in.

5.3Plans and prices, with a second approver

The plans shown on the Kiorly site (test.kiorly.com) are edited in the Kiorly admin, with no redeploy. Clinic, Pharmacy and Lab each have their own price rows; the platform keeps only the approval workflow.

  1. Open Kiorly plans in the Kiorly admin (or the Kiorex console; both editions show it). Ops, super admin, Kiorly ops and Kiorly admin may propose a price and see a preview.
  2. Read the advisory warnings in the preview (annual not above monthly, higher plan cheaper, large change, zero amount, currency change). They never block, but the approver sees them.
  3. A different super admin approves or rejects. The proposer can never approve their own proposal (the API, the database and the product all refuse).
  4. On approval the product publishes the price from the date you chose. Rejected and withdrawn proposals stay in the history.

Until a price is published the site says "Pricing to be announced". The seeded Egyptian Clinic prices are test points, not final, and their annual rows still need your decision on what "annual" means. No price has been published on kx-test.

5.4Join requests become draft onboarding

People who ask to join from the Kiorly site arrive as leads. Moving a lead to WON creates exactly one draft onboarding request, pre-filled with the kind, legal name, modules, market and the lead reference (no contact data). A draft is never a live organisation: a different super admin approves or rejects it, and approval still creates no organisation, enrolment code or Passport identity by itself. LOST creates nothing.

5.5Kiorly-only roles and dashboards

RoleConsoleWhat it can do

kiorly_opsKiorly admin onlyReviews markets, plans, leads and onboarding; proposes prices. Cannot approve and cannot see Kiorex-only data.
kiorly_adminKiorly admin onlySame as Kiorly ops with the Kiorly administration screens.

Every console role lands on its own dashboard, the menu shows only the screens the role may open, and a refused address shows a "No access" page. A Kiorly-only account is refused by the Kiorex console, and an account that holds Kiorex roles cannot also hold a Kiorly role (separate accounts, enforced). The super admin grants and revokes Kiorly roles in Passport with step-up and a reason; every change is audited and sessions end.
