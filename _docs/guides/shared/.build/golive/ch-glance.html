CHAPTER 01

At a glance

Taking Egypt live means one human action in the Kiorex super admin: recording launch gate evidence for the flag market.eg.live. Nothing in the code ever does this for you. Until you do it, the patient site and app say Kiorex is not live in Egypt and show no doctors; the day you do it, Egypt opens within seconds, and you can close it again with one click.

1.1The honest summary

QuestionAnswer
Which product owns the switch?Kiorex (ops console, which is the Kiorex super admin). The flag is market.eg.live, class LAUNCH_LOCKED, product "all".
Who can see it?ops_admin and super_admin (read-only for ops_admin).
Who can record the gate, switch it OFF, or revoke it?Only super_admin. The buttons are disabled for an ops-only account and the API answers 403.
Is a second person required?No. A second approver exists in the console only for money-path flags switched OFF at global or market scope. market.eg.live is not a money-path flag, so one super admin acts alone and the change applies immediately. Section 3.6 explains the two-person habit we recommend.
What evidence is required?Gate id, approver name, approval date and a link (http or https) to the approval document. The console never checks the document; it only records who said what and when.
Does Kiorly need the same?Yes, as a separate switch. Kiorly markets are launched from the Kiorly admin (Market launch) or the Clinic platform screen. It creates markets, records who did what (audit), and shows the Kiorex state read-only. It is deliberately not linked to the Kiorex gate. Part B (updated after ADR-060).
You do this yourself
By founder rule the gate is never recorded by code, by an agent or by a seed. This guide was produced against a private test stack and a sample approval; nothing was recorded on kx-test or anywhere shared. The real Egypt gate is yours to record, with the real approval document, when counsel and the market pack are ready (NEEDS_HUMAN rows 10 and 16).
The rule that does not change
Only the founder records the gate for market.eg.live, by hand, with the real approval document. No test run, seed, script or agent ever does it. On 28 Nov 2026 it is still not recorded on kx-test: Egypt shows "not live yet" to patients, by design.

1.2What is new in this edition (28 Nov 2026)

  • Hosts follow one pattern: <service>.test.<domain> (for example passport.test.kiorex.com, clinics.test.kiorly.com).
  • Dual sign-in for Clinic, Pharmacy and Lab: Kiorex Passport or e-mail and password, one shared sign-in card (chapter 05).
  • Plans and prices are managed in the Kiorly admin, and a price needs a second approver (chapter 05).
  • Join requests from the Kiorly site: a lead marked WON creates a draft onboarding request, never a live organisation.
  • Kiorly-only roles (kiorly_ops, kiorly_admin) and one dashboard per console role.
  • MFA policy is OFF by default for Pharmacy and Lab; only Passport ops turn it on, through a second approver.
  • New Kiorex landing and Passport register, Plus Jakarta Sans and Arabic spelling كيوريكس.
  • End-to-end result on kx-test: 311 passed, 0 failed, 11 skipped, 6 not run (chapter 06).
