CHAPTER 03

Part A: Kiorex, taking Egypt live

3.1What the gate is

market.eg.live is a launch-locked flag (Kiorex control plane, ADR-024). A launch-locked flag is effective only when two things are true: it is not switched OFF, and a human has recorded gate evidence for it. Until then its state reads ON, awaiting gate and the market is treated as not live. When the market is not live: Egypt is hidden from the sign-up country list and search, the patient site shows a banner, and profile, slots and booking answer 403 FEATURE_DISABLED. Existing organisations and data are never touched.

3.2Sign in as ops

Open the Ops console (kx-test: https://admin.test.kiorex.com). It sends you to Kiorex Passport. Use the ops account.

  1. On the Passport page enter your mobile number in international format and press Send code.
  2. Enter the six-digit code from the text message and press Verify.
  3. Ops roles always see Two-step verification: enter the six-digit code from your authenticator app (it changes every 30 seconds) and press Verify.
  4. If Passport offers to add a passkey, you may choose Not now. You land on Feature flags.

3.3Look before you leap

Still as ops, search the flag. In the Flags tab type market.eg.live in Search.

The Launch gates tab lists gates that have ids (for example GHV1 for home visits). market.eg.live does not appear there because it names no required gate id; it is recorded from the Flags tab, button Gate. Do not worry that it is missing.

Now look at the patient side while the gate is open. Open the patient site (kx-test: https://test.kiorex.com; the address in the registry is kiorex.marketplace).

3.4Sign in as super admin and record the gate

Sign out of the ops account (top right, Sign out) and repeat section 3.2 with the super admin account. Check the bottom-left label now shows both roles.

  1. Search market.eg.live in the Flags tab and press Gate on that row.
  2. The dialog Launch gate evidence market.eg.live opens. Its hint repeats what the flag needs: market pack certification and local counsel.
  3. Fill the four fields (table below). All four are required; the Record evidence button stays disabled until the link starts with http:// or https://.
  4. Read the four values once more, then press Record evidence. The toast "Gate evidence recorded" appears.

3.5Verify on the patient side

Verification has four checks. Do them in this order.

  1. Audit trail (Audit trail tab): a new row market.eg.live, event GATE, change AWAITING_GATE to GATE_SATISFIED, with the actor.
  2. Propagation tab: the registry version rose and the products catch up. Clinic, Pharmacy and Lab poll every 30 seconds (some are also pushed by webhook). STALE or NEVER_FETCHED simply means a product has not fetched yet or is not running; give it a minute.
  3. Public answer: GET /api/v1/markets now returns Egypt with live: true; ?all=true shows its state LIVE. On kx-test: https://api.test.kiorex.com/api/v1/markets (the CDN header gate may block visitors that are not on the allow-list).
  4. Patient site and app: the yellow bar is gone and doctors appear.

3.6One person or two

The platform enforces a second approver (maker-checker) only for money-path flags switched OFF at global or market scope, and for the commission policy. For market.eg.live a single super_admin records and revokes alone. Because you are the only super admin today, the control is you. Recommended habit, which is a practice and not a system rule:

  • Do the preparation as ops (collect approver, date and link, check the flag state), then the single write as super admin.
  • Write the approver as the person or body that actually approved (for example the counsel or the market-pack certifier), so the record does not read as "founder approved founder".
  • Ask a trusted person to look at the audit trail the same day. Anyone with the ops role can read it.

3.7Roll back

There are two ways to close Egypt again. Both act within seconds, both are audited, and neither deletes organisations, patients, bookings or any data ("OFF stops new actions only, never hides data").

3.8What this does not do

  • It does not create doctors. Providers appear from the Clinic sync; a market with no LIVE provider shows an empty list even when live.
  • It does not open other gates. Pharmacy orders (kiorex.pharmacy_orders), lab bookings (kiorex.lab_bookings) and telemedicine (kiorex.telemedicine) have their own evidence (NEEDS_HUMAN row 10).
  • It does not change commissions or money modes. Counsel sign-off on the commission policy is separate and still recorded only by a human.
  • It does not switch on Kiorly's own Egypt market. That is a different switch in a different product (Part B).
  • The screenshots show the Kiorex patient web. The patient app reads the same /markets answer and shows the same states.
