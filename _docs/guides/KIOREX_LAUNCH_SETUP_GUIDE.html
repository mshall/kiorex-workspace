QuestionAnswer
Can both apps share one Apple account and one Google account?Yes. One Apple Developer Program membership (one Team ID) and one Play Console developer account can publish any number of apps. Each app has its own bundle ID or package name.
Do I need two Apple IDs or two Google accounts, one per app?No. You need one organisation account on each store. Other people (a developer, a designer, me through your CI) are added as users with roles, never by sharing a password.
Can the main account be named "Kiorex"?Yes, with one condition. Apple shows the legal entity name as the seller. If your registered company is not named Kiorex, the store shows the company's legal name, and the apps themselves still show "Kiorex" and "Kiorly". Google lets you choose a public developer name, so "Kiorex" works there directly after verification.
Individual or organisation?Organisation, both stores. An individual account shows your personal name as the seller, cannot add team members properly, and on Google new individual accounts must run a 14-day closed test with 12 testers before production. Organisations are exempt from that test rule.
Do the apps share sign-in?Yes, both sign in through Passport, so a person with a patient account and a staff role signs in once to either app.
Do the website links (universal and app links) cover both?Yes. One Apple Team ID covers both apps. Each Android app has its own signing fingerprint. Kiorex links live on kiorex.com hosts, Kiorly links on app.<env>.kiorly.com.

What you need before you start

  • The legal company that will own the apps: exact registered name, address, phone, and a person with legal authority to sign (Apple checks).
  • A D-U-N-S number for that company. Both Apple and Google use it to verify an organisation. It is free: Apple has a lookup page (search "Apple D-U-N-S lookup"). If your company has none, requesting one takes days to a couple of weeks, so start this first.
  • Company email addresses on your domain, for example apple@kiorex.com and play@kiorex.com, plus support@ and privacy@. Use shared role mailboxes, not a personal inbox, so the accounts survive staff changes. If the domains have no mailboxes yet, create them first (any business email host works; I can recommend one).
  • A public website for the company and a privacy policy URL (both stores require one for a health app). Legal wording is for counsel; I will not write it.
  • A phone that can receive verification codes and a second device or authenticator app for two-factor sign-in.
Send me:

  • Which email addresses you created for Apple and for Google (the address only).

Apple Developer Program and App Store Connect

One organisation membership, two apps, one Team ID.
You99 USD per yearVerification: days to about two weeks

A. Enrol the organisation

  1. Create the company Apple Account. Go to account.apple.com and create an Apple Account with the role email (for example apple@kiorex.com). Turn on two-factor authentication with a real phone number.

  2. Start enrolment. Open developer.apple.com/programs/enroll and press Start Your Enrollment. Sign in with the company Apple Account.

  3. Choose the entity type: Organization. Not Individual. Enter the legal entity name exactly as registered, the D-U-N-S number, the headquarters address, phone and website.

  4. Confirm legal authority. The person enrolling must be able to bind the company. Apple may call the company phone to verify, so answer that number during working hours.

  5. Pay and wait. Pay 99 USD (it renews yearly; set a calendar reminder, a lapse removes the apps from sale). Apple emails when the membership is active.

B. Set up the team in App Store Connect

  1. Open App Store Connect: appstoreconnect.apple.com, sign in with the same account.

  2. Agreements. Business (older layout: Agreements, Tax, and Banking). The Free Apps agreement must show Active. Both apps are free downloads. If you later sell subscriptions or in-app purchases through Apple, the Paid Apps agreement, a bank account and tax forms are also required. Apple has rules on when digital purchases must use its payment system, so ask counsel before you sell provider subscriptions inside the iPhone app.

  3. Add people. Users and Access then the plus button. Give a developer the Developer or App Manager role. Keep Account Holder and Admin for the legal owner only.

  4. Register the two app identifiers. At developer.apple.com/account open Certificates, Identifiers and Profiles, then Identifiers, plus button, App IDs, App. Choose Explicit bundle ID com.kiorex.app, tick the capabilities Associated Domains and Push Notifications, and register. Repeat for com.kiorly.app. (Expo's EAS can also create these for you, but registering them yourself keeps the names exactly as above.)

  5. Create the two App Store records. In App Store Connect, Apps, plus button, New App: platform iOS, name Kiorex, primary language English (add Arabic later), bundle ID com.kiorex.app, SKU kiorex-ios. Repeat with name Kiorly, bundle ID com.kiorly.app, SKU kiorly-ios. App names are unique across the store: if "Kiorex" is already taken the form says so, and you can pick a variant such as "Kiorex Health" while the bundle ID stays the same.

  6. Prepare the listing items Apple asks for: privacy policy URL, support URL, a description in English and Arabic, screenshots, age rating, the App Privacy questionnaire (health and contact data are collected), and a review account. Create a dedicated reviewer account on the real system with its own unique password, never one of the seeded demo passwords. A health app must also offer in-app account deletion; I will confirm both apps have it before the first review.

C. Find your Team ID

  1. Go to developer.apple.com/account and open Membership details (left menu, or the profile menu). Copy the Team ID: 10 letters and digits.

Send me:
  • The Team ID.
  • Confirmation that the two bundle IDs exist and the two app records are created (or the name Apple refused).

Google Play Console

One developer account named Kiorex, two apps.
You25 USD, one timeIdentity check: days

A. Create the developer account

  1. Use a Google account for the company (for example play@kiorex.com on a Google Workspace or any Google account), with two-step verification on.

  2. Open play.google.com/console/signup. Choose Organization as the account type.

  3. Developer name: type Kiorex. This is the public name on every app page. Add the contact email, phone, website and the D-U-N-S number.

  4. Pay the 25 USD registration fee, then complete identity verification (company documents and a phone and email check). The console is limited until Google finishes.

B. Create the two apps

  1. In the console press Create app. App name Kiorex, default language English, type App, Free, accept the declarations. Repeat with the name Kiorly.

  2. Package names are set by the first build you upload: com.kiorex.app and com.kiorly.app. They can never change, so I will confirm them in the builds before the first upload.

  1. Open the app, then Test and release, Setup, App signing (older layout: Release, Setup, App signing).

  2. Under App signing key certificate, copy the SHA-256 certificate fingerprint. This one is for the Play-installed app. Also copy the Upload key certificate SHA-256: builds installed directly (internal testing) use it.

  3. Repeat for the second app.

Send me:
  • For each app: the App signing key SHA-256 and the Upload key SHA-256.
  • The confirmed developer name once Google verifies it.

Domains and DNS

Point test.kiorex.com and test.kiorly.com at AWS. Nothing else changes.
You10 minutes eachAbout 1 USD/month on AWS side
i

What this does. I create two small DNS zones in AWS (test.kiorex.com, test.kiorly.com). Each zone has four nameserver names. You add them at the registrar as NS records for the host test. Your websites, email and anything else on the main domains stay exactly as they are.

!

Do not change the domain's main nameservers. Only add records. Changing the main nameservers moves the whole domain and can take your email and websites offline.

Step 5.1: tell me where each domain lives

Send me:
  • Which registrar holds kiorex.com and which holds kiorly.com (domain.com or Namecheap).
  • Whether each domain uses the registrar's own nameservers (the default) or a custom DNS service.

I will then create the zones and send you four values per domain, written like ns-123.awsdns-45.com.

Step 5.2: add the records

If the domain is on Namecheap

  1. Sign in, open Domain List, press Manage next to the domain.

  2. Open the Advanced DNS tab. The domain must be on Namecheap BasicDNS (or PremiumDNS). If it says Custom DNS, add the records at that DNS provider instead.

  3. Press Add New Record. Type NS Record, Host test, Value: the first AWS name (no trailing spaces), TTL Automatic. Save with the green check.

  4. Repeat for all four values, so the host test has four NS records.

If the domain is on domain.com

  1. Sign in, open My Domains (Domain Manager) and select the domain.

  2. Open DNS & Nameservers, then DNS Records. Use Add DNS Record.

  3. Type NS, Name/Host test, Value the first AWS name, TTL the default. Save.

  4. Repeat for the other three. The domain must be using domain.com's own nameservers for these records to take effect.

Step 5.3: check it worked

Wait 5 to 30 minutes, then run on your computer. The four AWS names should come back:

dig NS test.kiorex.com +short
dig NS test.kiorly.com +short

Tell me when it is done. I verify, request the free certificates (AWS ACM), and the stack starts serving on the real names such as passport.test.kiorex.com and clinics.test.kiorly.com. Your existing Clinic address keeps working.

Email with Amazon SES

Appointment, invoice and sign-in emails from your own domains.
You (or I, if you give me AWS access for this)Approval about a dayAbout 0.10 USD per 1,000 emails

  1. Open the AWS Console in the same account as your Clinic, switch the region to Europe (Frankfurt) eu-central-1, search Amazon SES.

  2. Identities, Create identity, type Domain, enter kiorly.com, keep Easy DKIM (RSA 2048) on, create. Repeat for kiorex.com.

  3. SES shows three CNAME records per domain. Add them at the registrar's main DNS (names like abc123._domainkey.kiorly.com). Wait until the identity shows Verified.

  4. Add one TXT record per domain for DMARC: name _dmarc, value v=DMARC1; p=none; rua=mailto:dmarc@kiorex.com. Start with p=none and tighten later.

  5. SES starts in sandbox (can only email verified addresses). Press Request production access in the account dashboard: mail type Transactional, your website, and a short description: appointment confirmations, reminders, receipts and sign-in codes to people who requested them, with unsubscribe and bounce handling.

Send me:
  • Confirmation both domains show Verified and production access is approved.
  • The sender addresses you want (for example no-reply@kiorly.com).

Handing me secrets safely

They go from your keyboard to AWS Secrets Manager, never through chat or Git.

For each secret I will give you the exact name. You paste the value into a hidden prompt on your own computer (the AWS command line is already set up there):

read -s -p "Paste the value, then Enter: " V; echo
aws secretsmanager create-secret --region eu-central-1 \
  --name "<the name I gave you>" --secret-string "$V"
unset V
  • The value is never printed or saved in your shell history.
  • If the secret already exists, use put-secret-value with --secret-id instead of create-secret.
  • I read secrets only by reference at run time. I never see or print the value.

Questions you may have

Why one Apple team for both apps? Is it allowed to publish unrelated brands?
Yes. A team can publish any apps it owns. Kiorex and Kiorly are two products of the same company, so one membership and one Team ID is the normal setup, and it simplifies links and push keys.
What if the company is not named Kiorex?
Apple shows the registered legal name as the seller. Changing a company's legal name takes a legal process, and Apple must re-verify. Your options: keep the legal name as seller (app names still say Kiorex and Kiorly), or register a company whose legal name includes Kiorex before you enrol. The Google developer name can be Kiorex either way.
Can I start the stores before DNS and the cloud are ready?
Yes, and you should. The verification waits are independent of everything else we build.
Do I pay Apple or Google per app?
No. Apple is 99 USD a year for the whole team, Google is a 25 USD one-time fee for the whole account. Neither charges per app.
What about selling provider subscriptions inside the app?
Both stores have rules on when digital purchases must use their payment systems, with exceptions for services consumed outside the app and for business customers. This depends on how the subscription is sold, so it is a question for counsel before the first review. I have not assumed an answer.
Will you stop me from doing something risky?
Yes. Real-money payments, real patient-data moves between environments and the counsel sign-off are blocked by design until you and counsel say otherwise, and I ask before promoting any environment.
