## 5. Financial model updates for KX-13

### 5.1 New inputs (all `ASSUMPTION` until pilot data)

| Input | Symbol | Starting value | Source of truth later |
|---|---|---|---|
| Active clinic tenants (existing) | T | KX-14 §07: M6 341, M12 857 | KX-13 |
| Share of tenants with at least one LIVE listing | L | M6 20%, M12 45% | ops providers page |
| Listed providers per listing tenant | d | 1.5 | provider sync |
| Attended new patients per listed provider per month | a | M6 4, M12 8 (KX-14 gate for opening the next cluster is 12) | commission charges plus decisions |
| Provider-led share of attended new patients | p | 35% to 50% (founder hypothesis below) | `AttributionDecision` |
| Fee per attended new patient, net of waivers | f | EGP 40 (indexed) | `KiorexCommissionRate` |
| Dispute and reversal rate | r | 4% | `CommissionDispute` |
| Unattributed share treated as marketplace | u | 10% of bookings | decisions |
| Search to booking conversion | c1 | >= 8% (KX-14 gate) | funnel |
| Booking to attended | c2 | 75% | commission data |
| Patient marketing spend | S_p | KX-14 §09 "Patient acquisition 30%" of S&M | |
| Content and SEO spend | S_c | 12% of S&M | |
| WhatsApp marketing messages per month and price | m, w | Meta rate card by category (S14), not yet modelled | Meta pricing page |
| Reward cost per qualified referral | q | open (founder, counsel) | |
| Referral breakage (expired or unused) | b | 30% | ledger |

### 5.2 Formulas

```
ListedProviders(t)      = T(t) * L(t) * d
ANP_total(t)            = ListedProviders(t) * a(t)                      // attended new patients
ANP_fee_bearing(t)      = ANP_total(t) * (1 - p(t))                      // provider-led patients carry no Kiorex fee
KiorexFeeRevenue(t)     = ANP_fee_bearing(t) * f * (1 - r)               // gross booking value is NOT revenue
BookingsNeeded(t)       = ANP_total(t) / c2
SearchersNeeded(t)      = BookingsNeeded(t) / c1                         // only for the marketplace-origin share
BlendedPatientCAC(t)    = (S_p(t) + S_c(t)) / ANP_total(t)
PaidCAC(c)              = Spend(c) / ANP_attributed_to(c)
ReferralCost(t)         = QualifiedReferrals(t) * q * (1 - b)
ROAS(c)                 = KiorexFeeRevenue_attributed(c) / Spend(c)
MER(t)                  = KiorexFeeRevenue(t) / TotalMarketing(t)
MessagingCost(t)        = sum over categories (messages * delivered price)   // S14
```

Key modelling consequences: (1) Every point of provider-led share removes EGP 40 per patient from revenue while *increasing* provider adoption; the model must carry `p` explicitly. (2) Kiorex earns once per patient per provider network (24-month lookback), so patient LTV for marketing purposes is about the first-visit fee plus Pay margin, not visit count. (3) GBV, deposits held and refunds are cash-flow items, not revenue; deposits sit in the held-deposit ledger (never commingled).

### 5.3 Pressure test of KX-14 §07 against a bottom-up (arithmetic on the assumptions above, not a forecast)

| Month | Tenants T | L | d | Listed providers | a | ANP total | p | Fee-bearing ANP | Fee revenue / month (f=40, r=4%) | KX-14 "attended Kiorex visits / month" |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| M6 | 341 | 20% | 1.5 | 102 | 4 | 409 | 50% | 205 | EGP 7,872 | 2,900 |
| M12 | 857 | 45% | 1.5 | 578 | 8 | 4,628 | 40% | 2,777 | EGP 106,637 | 13,667 |

Reading: even with generous listing rates, the bottom-up lands near **14% (M6) and 34% (M12)** of KX-14's attended-visit targets. They reconcile only if the KX-14 series counts repeat visits and visits that came through provider links, or if listing rates and patients per provider are far higher than assumed (at the KX-14 cluster gate of 12 attended new patients per listed provider, M12 needs about 1,140 listed providers to reach 13,667, which exceeds 857 active tenants). **Action: KX-13 must split "attended Kiorex visits" into new, returning and provider-led, and re-base revenue on fee-bearing ANP.** This is a modelling finding, not a statement that the target is wrong.
