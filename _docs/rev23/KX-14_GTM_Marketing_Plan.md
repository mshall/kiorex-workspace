## 01 Positioning
Two audiences, two promises, one proof point.

### Kiorly — for providers (B2B)
- Promise: run your clinic — or your home-care practice — from your phone, in Arabic, free to start; and pay nothing extra when Kiorex fills your empty slots.
- Proof: pay the higher of subscription or commission, never both. No charge for no-shows. Your own booking link is commission-free. Care-plan follow-ups are never charged.
- Tagline tests: 'Kiorly — your clinic, organised. Free while Kiorex keeps you busy.' · Kiorly Nurse: 'Your visits, your patients, your pay — organised.'

### Kiorex — for patients (B2C)
- Promise: book verified care with a real slot in under two minutes — at the clinic or at home — and know the fee upfront.
- Proof: only verified providers (checked for their profession); reviews only from completed visits; instant confirmation from the provider's own system; see who is coming and when.
- Tagline test: 'Kiorex — real doctors, real slots, real reviews.'

## 03 Beachhead: density beats coverage
A marketplace is liquid neighbourhood by neighbourhood. We launch in two Cairo/Giza micro-clusters and only expand when liquidity thresholds are met.

| Cluster (candidates) | Why |
|---|---|
| Nasr City + Heliopolis | Highest lab and clinic density in Cairo (POI data: Nasr City and Heliopolis lead lab counts) |
| New Cairo (incl. Fifth Settlement) | Young families, high smartphone use, new clinics opening |
| Mohandessin + Dokki (Giza) | Dense specialist streets |
| Maadi, 6th of October, Alexandria | Wave 2 after M9 |

Liquidity threshold to open the next cluster:
- >=150 LIVE doctors across top 8 specialties
- Median next-available slot <=48 h
- Search → booking conversion >=8%
- >=12 attended Kiorex patients / listed provider / month

Home visits open per cluster only when >=50 home-enabled providers (doctors, physiotherapists, agencies) cover its districts (KX03 gate). [exact comparator symbol lost; ">=" assumed]

Cluster playbook (six steps): 01 Recruit anchors (30 respected clinics) → 02 Fill specialties (top 8 covered) → 03 Drive demand (local SEO · Maps · social) → 04 Measure liquidity (slots · conversion) → 05 Open Kiorex closed beta (M4) → 06 Add home care (physio · agencies · home visits) → Next cluster (repeat).

## 06 Competitive landscape
We respect incumbents and win on economics, trust and workflow depth.

| Player | What they do | How providers pay | Our angle |
|---|---|---|---|
| Vezeeta | Doctor booking + practice software; also pharmacy; operating a medical building in New Cairo (2026) | Transaction fee per booking varying by specialty and location; SaaS subscription option | Never pay for no-shows; software free above crossover; verified-visit reviews |
| Clinic EMR/PMS vendors | Local and regional clinic software | Licence or subscription | Free Lite tier, Arabic-first, built-in demand |
| Chefaa, Yodawy | E-pharmacy / pharmacy benefit networks | Order commissions; payer contracts | Run the pharmacy itself (Kiorly Pharmacy) with true stock |
| Altibbi, Shezlong and telehealth apps | Teleconsultation, mental health | Per consultation | In-person and home first; telehealth later via Kiorly Clinic records |
| Home-nursing operators (e.g. 7keema, Tabibi 24/7) | Employ or dispatch nurses and run home-care services | Patients pay the operator | We do not operate care: agencies are Kiorly Nurse customers; independent nurses get software and (when allowed) demand |
| Lab chains' own apps | Booking within one chain | Own channel | Multi-lab marketplace + LIMS for independents |

## 10 Brand voice and messaging rules
- Arabic first, Egyptian dialect for patient copy, Modern Standard Arabic for clinical/legal; English second.
- Plain promises we can prove; no 'AI' claims beyond the AI03 registry; no superlatives about clinical outcomes. Providers are heroes, not inventory: never rank by payment without a label; never shame low ratings publicly.
- Home care copy never promises response times we do not control and never implies Kiorex employs or sends the nurse.
- Every campaign approved by the CMO for clinical accuracy.
