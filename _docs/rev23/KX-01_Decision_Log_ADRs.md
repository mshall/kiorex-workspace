## 01 How decisions are recorded
Short, numbered, immutable. Superseding a decision means a new ADR that references the old one. Process: Context (problem + evidence) → Options (2 real alternatives) → Decision (one sentence) → Consequences (cost · risk · rollback) → Status (Proposed → Accepted → Superseded). Accepted decisions are binding for all volumes and code. Proposed are validated in pilot. A volume that contradicts an Accepted ADR is wrong; raise a revision. ADR IDs are referenced in commit messages and Docs/.
Counters: ACCEPTED 32 (binding) · PROPOSED 5 (ADR-033, ADR-035 and ADR-036 added 23 Nov; validate in pilot) · V2-DRAFT ITEMS SUPERSEDED 9 (see §04) · OPEN QUESTIONS 17 (owners named).

## 03 Decisions in detail
**ADR001** Accepted · Strategy. Why: Decouples failure domains and lets each product be sold alone. Replaces the 34-module all-in-one model. Consequence: Separate repos, deployables and databases per product.
**ADR002** Accepted · Brand. Why: Patients and providers buy for different reasons; one name per audience keeps marketing spend focused. Consequence: Kiorly Clinic / Kiorly Nurse / Kiorly Pharmacy / Kiorly Lab; singular names.
**ADR003** Accepted · Architecture. Why: Shipped, tested and enforced by module-boundary tests; extraction path documented (MODULAR_MONOLITH). Consequence: Extract a context only when scale or team ownership demands it.
**ADR004** Accepted · Architecture. Why: Plans, entitlements, subscriptions, credit wallet, settlement and market packs are already proven in Kiorly; Pharmacy and Lab reuse them. Consequence: Publish @kiorly/commerce-kernel from clinics-management; versioned, semver, contract-tested. The same pattern publishes @kiorly/field-visit (ADR030).
**ADR005** Accepted · Data. Why: Avoids double-booking; Kiorex keeps booking intent and attribution only. Consequence: Kiorex books via holds + Idempotency-Key; never writes appointments directly. Applies equally to home visits.
**ADR006** Accepted · Integration. Why: Already built (KX01/KX05); simple, auditable, works across clouds. Consequence: OpenAPI pinned in both CIs (NH019); event bus only if fan-out exceeds 3 consumers.
**ADR007** Accepted · Commercial. Why: Aligns price with delivered value; resistant to fee-inflation disputes; easy to explain. Consequence: EGP 40 placeholder; per specialty, profession and service-location override; benchmark guardrail at 50% of lowest competitor.
**ADR008** Accepted · Commercial. Why: Makes Kiorly 'free' for providers Kiorex keeps busy — the core wedge against incumbents. Consequence: CR06 settlement engine; informational offset line.
**ADR009** Accepted · Commercial. Why: Zero-risk entry for single practitioners; marketplace supply growth. Consequence: Minimum invoice EGP 100, carry-forward; unpaid → listing paused, Kiorly keeps working.
**ADR010** Accepted · Commercial. Why: Speeds supply acquisition in Egypt while filtering inactive sign-ups. Consequence: 500 clinics; ≥20 checked-in visits in 30 days else 90-day trial.
**ADR011** Accepted · Compliance. Kiorly does no tax-authority ETA integration. Why: Decided after legal review; clinics issue their own receipts. Consequence: FD02 removed fiscal modules; printouts are payment records only.
**ADR012** Accepted · Identity. Why: Do not hand-roll identity; Kiorly launch must not wait. Consequence: Kiorly keeps its auth at launch behind an OIDC-ready seam; providers federate in phase 2.
**ADR013** Accepted · Privacy. Why: Identity roles must not imply chart access. Consequence: Kiorex Account holds subject, credentials, memberships only — no profession, licence or address claims.
**ADR014** Accepted · Platform. Why: New country without code forks; already implemented (Market pack, commercialConfig). Consequence: Market go-live template + certification checklist; packs now also carry the scope-of-practice matrix and district lists for coverage zones.
**ADR015** Accepted · Infrastructure. Why: No in-country hyperscaler region; recorded government approval path (SC01). Consequence: platform/data-cells.json; optional future cell when available.
**ADR016** Accepted · AI. Why: Speed to launch with redaction and consent; cost and control later. Consequence: Provider abstraction; market-specific redaction; plan-gated quotas.
**ADR017** Accepted · Sequencing. Why: Supply and compliance first; marketplace consumes their APIs. Consequence: Pharmacy pilot M13, Lab pilot M19; marketplace verticals follow GA. Kiorly Nurse follows the same pattern (software pilot before Kiorex home-nursing category).
**ADR018** Accepted · Distribution. Why: Apple 5.1.1(ix) requires a legal entity for health apps; multiple accounts add no value. Consequence: Kiorex app + Kiorly app (edition-aware for Kiorly Nurse); field apps only on demand.
**ADR019** Accepted · Brand. Why: Session-cookie isolation and focused SEO. Consequence: Marketing in paths, apps on subdomains (incl. nurse.kiorly.com); kiorly.link for booking links.
**ADR020** Accepted · Trust. Why: Verified-only reviews are a differentiator and a moderation safeguard. Consequence: REVIEW_INVITATION_ELIGIBLE event on COMPLETED (KX08).
**ADR021** Proposed · Commercial. Why: Adds revenue without compromising trust. Consequence: Launch after marketplace liquidity; ranking explanation page.
**ADR022** Proposed · Finance. Why: Seed lasts to M27 in base case; raise from strength. Consequence: Gates in KX13 §10; raise window M15–M20.
**ADR023** Accepted · Platform. Why: Founder decision 19 Nov 2026 — build every module, release it ON, and keep operational control centrally without deploys. Consequence: Flag control plane in kiorex-platform; global · market · organization scopes; any OFF wins; universal OFF contract; Kiorly FF01…06.
**ADR024** Accepted · Compliance. Why: Keeps the default-ON model while preventing regulated features from going live before approval. Consequence: Pharmacy orders, lab bookings, telemedicine, AI navigation, AI receptionist, specialty templates, new markets — and from v2.3 home visits (GHV1/GHV2), physiotherapy category (GPR1), nursing care plans and MAR (GKN1), individual nurse listing (GKN2).
**ADR025** Accepted · Clinical safety. Why: A switch that can disable Rx checks, QC or audit is itself a hazard. Consequence: Fixed list in KX08; CI rejects flag keys on safety paths. v2.3 adds scope-of-practice enforcement, profession verification, home-visit safety timer/SOS/unsafe-abort, address masking and MAR checks.
**ADR026** Accepted · Engineering. Why: Assessment (KX22): kiorex-backend is an untested TypeORM microservice prototype with Stripe/Twilio; kiorex-web-app is UI with dummy data; kiorex-ai-services is empty; backend-documentation describes an unbuilt architecture. Consequence: Build kiorex-platform from the Kiorly skeleton; harvest patient UI only; secret-scan then archive all four repos.
**ADR027** Accepted · Engineering. Why: Keeps repos independently deployable while reusing the kernel and contracts. Consequence: @kiorly/commerce-kernel, @kiorly/field-visit, @kiorex/contracts; semver; 90-day dual support on majors.
**ADR028** Accepted · Infrastructure. Why: Founder decision 19 Nov 2026 — stay on AWS but be able to switch cloud without a rewrite; OpenTofu is open source (MPL 2.0) and free. Consequence: CDK retired before the Kiorly pilot; encrypted S3 state; app adapters for storage, secrets, email; no AWS SDK outside adapters (KX21 §01).
**ADR029 · Profession is a first-class attribute of every clinician** Accepted · Clinical / Data. Why: Physiotherapists and nurses are licensed under different laws and syndicates from doctors (physiotherapy: Law 3 of 1985 [extraction garbled]) and may not do the same things — they must never issue a drug prescription. Today every clinician is implicitly a doctor. Decision: Clinician.profession ∈ PHYSICIAN, DENTIST, PHYSIOTHERAPIST, NURSE, extensible by market pack; a scope-of-practice matrix maps profession × capability. Consequence: @RequiresScope guard (never a flag); verification per profession (PRVER); Kiorex care-type categories; existing clinicians migrate to PHYSICIAN/DENTIST. Rollback: none needed — additive.
**ADR030 · Service location is set per visit type** Accepted · Product / Data. Why: Doctors, physiotherapists and nurses all do home visits; building it per profession would triple the work and split the slot engine. Options: separate home-care product vs. a property of the visit type — the latter chosen. Decision: VisitType.serviceLocation ∈ IN_CLINIC, HOME (VIDEO reserved). Consequence: Slot engine gains coverage zones, travel buffers and home caps; Kiorly remains system of record (ADR005); field logic published as @kiorly/field-visit and reused by Kiorly Lab home collection; home-visit safety controls are never flags; launch-locked behind GHV1/GHV2.
**ADR031 · Kiorly Nurse is an edition of Kiorly Clinic** Accepted · Architecture / Brand. Why: Most of what a nurse or agency needs (Estimated ≈80%) already exists in Kiorly Clinic. A separate product would fork scheduling, payments, messaging and the Kiorex connector. Decision: Organization.edition ∈ CLINIC, NURSE; the NURSE edition has its own name, navigation, terminology, plans and modules (care plans, nursing records, vitals, MAR, shifts, dispatch) and hides doctor-only modules. Consequence: Same repository, API, database, kernel and connector; edition set at sign-up, changed only by the super admin; PRD is KX23.
**ADR032 · Individual nurses and agencies are both tenants** Accepted · Commercial / Compliance. Why: Home nursing in Egypt is delivered by companies and by freelance nurses; excluding individuals leaves most supply out, but listing them to patients without a legal basis is a safety and regulatory risk. Decision: A solo nurse is an organization of one (Nurse Lite / Nurse Pro); agencies use Nurse Agency. Software use is open to both from day one; Kiorex listing of individual nurses is launch-locked (GKN2). Consequence: nurse.individual_kiorex_listing flag; stricter solo verification (ID selfie match, criminal-record certificate — Q8); agencies listed after GHV1/GHV2.

**ADR033 · One Kiorly staff app, tiered mobile entitlements, safety never paywalled** Proposed (drafted 20 Nov 2026, `_docs/mobile/ADR-033_One_Kiorly_App_Tiered_Mobile.md`; becomes Accepted when the founder signs it). Amends the consequence of ADR018 (one store account per platform stays; the Kiorly app is the staff app and hosts the Clinic, Pharmacy and Lab modules and the Nurse edition) and extends ADR025. Decision: one Expo app for provider staff; modules appear only from memberships, roles and per-organization entitlements; entitlements (who may use) are separate from flags (whether a capability exists), both evaluated server-side; safety capabilities (critical lab value, controlled-drug discrepancy, nurse SOS and missed safety check, interaction and allergy warnings, recalls, cold chain) reach every plan including Lite, with no flag and no paid entitlement, guarded by a registry and a CI test; no commerce on iOS (neutral locked text, no prices); device-bound sessions and app lock; offline queue with per-action conflict policy, clinical sign-offs never queued; split into branded builds stays an option by build configuration only. **What is built (23 Nov 2026):** the app ships as **Kiorly** (named Kiorly Work until 22 Nov, Kiorly Team on 22 to 23 Nov; final name Kiorly from 23 Nov, ADR037; repo `kiorly-team`, being renamed to `kiorly`; bundle id `com.kiorly.app`, scheme `kiorly`) with sign-in entries Clinic, Nurse, Pharmacy and Lab, gated today by a single `mobile_staff_app` entitlement (ON for Pro and Enterprise plans, OFF for Basic/Lite) plus role-based modules from the server; a Basic organization gets a real slice (safety-alert inbox, remote sign-out, upgrade request) and `FEATURE_DISABLED` on every other `/mobile/*` route. The tiered `mobile.*` keys, the safety registry CI test, multi-organization switching and the Passport public client `kiorly-app` are **Planned**, not built. **Name:** final name is plain **Kiorly** (ADR037, 23 Nov 2026), which supersedes the naming in this ADR's earlier text and the interim name Kiorly Team; the tiering decision itself still awaits sign-off (open question Q14).
**ADR034 · The notification layer is a package, not a service** Accepted (founder decision recorded 23 Nov 2026 in `kiorex-contracts/docs/NOTIFICATION_LAYER.md`). Why: a central notification service would make every Kiorly product depend on Kiorex being up (violates principle P1), would carry PHI-adjacent data across products, and adds a service, a database and an on-call rota; the Kiorly pipeline already exists per product. Decision: intent, template registry, PHI guard, routing and fallback, consent and quiet-hours gate, status model and audit shape ship as `@kiorex/contracts/notify` (contracts 1.7.0, pure TypeScript, no I/O); each product injects its own channel adapters, preference store, audit sink, dispatch store and clock. Consequence: migration is one repo at a time (platform done; then Passport, Lab, Pharmacy, Clinic); a central service may be reconsidered only if cross-product operations justify it; Super Admin runtime template and routing control is a Proposed later layer (KX24 §10). Supersedes nothing; clarifies the "no service" row in §04. The Drive draft of KX24 (22 Nov) numbered this decision ADR-033; it is renumbered here because ADR-033 already exists.
**ADR035 · One provider adapter contract for every outbound dependency** Proposed (implemented in kiorex-platform, kiorex-account, clinics-management, kiorly-pharmacy and kiorly-lab; awaiting founder sign-off). Why: founder decision 19 Nov 2026 to run every provider on mock adapters until a go-live pass, and to make going live a configuration change. Decision: each kind (SMS, EMAIL, WHATSAPP, PAYMENT, PUSH, STORAGE, CONSENT, AI) has a port with a `mock` and a `real` implementation selected by `<KIND>_MODE`, an env contract `<KIND>_API_URL`, `<KIND>_API_KEY`, `<KIND>_WEBHOOK_SECRET`, timeouts, retries with backoff on network, timeout, 429 and 5xx (never 4xx) with the same `Idempotency-Key` on every attempt, error mapping without vendor body or recipient, HMAC-SHA256 webhooks over the raw body with 5 min tolerance and de-duplication, forward-only statuses, startup refusal when `MODE=real` lacks a variable (names only), dev-only simulators that answer 404 in production, and one mapping file per kind as the only vendor-specific code. Consequence: only vendor URLs, credentials, template approvals and wire-format mapping remain for go-live; see `_docs/PROVIDER_GO_LIVE.md` and KX24 §05. Open: the consent registry wire format is an assumption; push has no HTTP provider in the clinic and platform repos.
**ADR037 · The staff app is named Kiorly** Accepted (founder decision 23 Nov 2026; App Store name checked). Supersedes the naming in ADR033 and the interim names Kiorly Work and Kiorly Team; the tiering and safety decisions of ADR033 are untouched. Why: three names in four days hurt the store listing and the team's vocabulary; the Kiorly name is the provider brand and the staff app is its mobile home. Decision: display name `Kiorly`; store title `Kiorly: Clinic, Pharmacy & Lab`; "by Kiorex" appears only on the splash screen and in About, never in the icon label or the store name; icon is the Kiorex mark with the Kiorly accent (not a product colour); modules inside are Clinic, Nurse, Pharmacy and Lab; the web products keep their `Kiorly Clinic by Kiorex` lockups; bundle id `com.kiorly.app`, scheme `kiorly`, header `X-Kiorly-Client: kiorly/<version>` (legacy `kiorly-team/*` and `kiorly-work/*` still accepted by all backends); Arabic name كيورلي. Consequence: docs, brand notes and PDFs use the new name; the repo folder `kiorly-team` is renamed to `kiorly` by the lead and the code and config rename is done by other agents; Apple and Google organization registration and the Passport public client stay NEEDS_HUMAN (the ids above are placeholders until registered). Rollback: renaming is cheap before store registration and costly after.
**ADR038 · Kiorly Home: one web sign-in for the Kiorly products** Accepted (founder decision, 23 Nov 2026; mirrors the Kiorly mobile app of ADR-037). Why: provider staff use Clinic, Nurse, Pharmacy and Lab, and today each web product has its own sign-in; the mobile app already presents one sign-in and then the workspaces. Decision: (1) A static Vite React app, Kiorly Home (`kiorex-account/apps/kiorly-home`), is the sign-in entry at `https://www.kiorly.com/signin` (kiorly.com redirects to www). The person signs in first with Kiorex Passport (phone OTP or passkey) as the public PKCE client `kiorly-home`; only then does Home call the authenticated `GET /api/workspaces` on Passport and show the workspaces as cards: Kiorly Clinic by Kiorex (Teal #0D9488), Kiorly Nurse (an EDITION of Clinic: routes into the Clinic app with edition NURSE, host nurse.kiorly.com), Kiorly Pharmacy by Kiorex (Green #16A34A), Kiorly Lab by Kiorex (Violet #7C3AED), using the existing lockups in `_brand/` (never redrawn). Last used is remembered and shown first; exactly one workspace skips the picker; a deep link (`/signin?to=pharmacy`, `/signin/lab`) goes straight to the product; "Switch workspace" (`?switch=1`) always shows the picker; organisations within a product keep the product's own organisation picker. (2) No enumeration: Home never asks for a phone number and never calls the workspace API before the IdP has verified the code, so nothing about which products a number belongs to is revealed before verification. (3) People with no workspace see Create or join for Clinic, Nurse, Pharmacy and Lab, linking to each product's existing sign-up (Clinic and Pharmacy `/signup`, Nurse `/signup?edition=nurse`, Lab `/onboarding`). (4) Patients are not part of it (they stay on Kiorex). (5) Products stay separate deployments with their own URLs and keep working on their own, without Kiorex: Home only redirects to `<product>/login?sso=passport&from=kiorly-home[&edition=NURSE]` and the product completes its OWN silent Passport code flow with the existing SSO session; no shared database and no source imports between products. (6) Workspace source: Passport organisation memberships (product from the organisation's `product_refs` key, default Clinic, edition from the organisation). When the Passport-to-staff links in the three product APIs are authoritative they replace this derivation behind the same response shape (seam, KX-08). (7) Sign-out from Home ends the Passport session by RP-initiated logout with `id_token_hint` and returns to Home; products register Home's `/signin` as an allowed post-logout URI so they can return to Home too. (8) Domains are configuration only: `kiorex-account/config/domains.json` is the single table per environment (local, staging, production); the Passport client registrations, the workspace endpoint URLs, the Home build, app-link files and the OpenTofu module `kiorly-domains` derive from or are asserted against it by tests that fail if a production entry contains localhost or an unlisted host. Production hosts: www.kiorly.com (Home), clinic.kiorly.com (app.kiorly.com stays an alias), nurse.kiorly.com, pharmacy.kiorly.com, lab.kiorly.com; Passport stays on account.kiorex.com. Consequence: one more small Passport client and one more static site; the product-side change is only a login page that honours `sso=passport` (owned by the product repos). Not verified: any deploy, DNS, certificates or the production Passport client (NEEDS_HUMAN: Docs/NEEDS_HUMAN.md in kiorex-account); product-side `sso=passport` auto-start was not yet merged in the three product repos at the time of writing.
