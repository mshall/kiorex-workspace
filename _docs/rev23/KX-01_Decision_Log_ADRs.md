## 01 How decisions are recorded
Short, numbered, immutable. Superseding a decision means a new ADR that references the old one. Process: Context (problem + evidence) → Options (2 real alternatives) → Decision (one sentence) → Consequences (cost · risk · rollback) → Status (Proposed → Accepted → Superseded). Accepted decisions are binding for all volumes and code. Proposed are validated in pilot. A volume that contradicts an Accepted ADR is wrong; raise a revision. ADR IDs are referenced in commit messages and Docs/.
Counters (Rev 2.3 line, not recomputed; ADR-039 and ADR-040 are Accepted and are not in this count; ADR-041 to ADR-047 are Proposed and are not in it either): ACCEPTED 32 (binding) · PROPOSED 5 (ADR-033, ADR-035 and ADR-036 added 23 Nov; validate in pilot) · V2-DRAFT ITEMS SUPERSEDED 9 (see §04) · OPEN QUESTIONS 17 (owners named).

## 03 Decisions in detail
**ADR001** Accepted · Strategy. Why: Decouples failure domains and lets each product be sold alone. Replaces the 34-module all-in-one model. Consequence: Separate repos, deployables and databases per product.
**ADR002** Accepted · Brand. Why: Patients and providers buy for different reasons; one name per audience keeps marketing spend focused. Consequence: Kiorly Clinic / Kiorly Nurse / Kiorly Pharmacy / Kiorly Lab; singular names.
**ADR003** Accepted · Architecture. Why: Shipped, tested and enforced by module-boundary tests; extraction path documented (MODULAR_MONOLITH). Consequence: Extract a context only when scale or team ownership demands it.
**ADR004** Accepted · Architecture. Why: Plans, entitlements, subscriptions, credit wallet, settlement and market packs are already proven in Kiorly; Pharmacy and Lab reuse them. Consequence: Publish @kiorly/commerce-kernel from clinics-management; versioned, semver, contract-tested. The same pattern publishes @kiorly/field-visit (ADR030).
**ADR005** Accepted · Data. Why: Avoids double-booking; Kiorex keeps booking intent and attribution only. Consequence: Kiorex books via holds + Idempotency-Key; never writes appointments directly. Applies equally to home visits.
**ADR006** Accepted · Integration. Why: Already built (KX01/KX05); simple, auditable, works across clouds. Consequence: OpenAPI pinned in both CIs (NH019); event bus only if fan-out exceeds 3 consumers.
**ADR007** Accepted · Commercial. Why: Aligns price with delivered value; resistant to fee-inflation disputes; easy to explain. Consequence: EGP 40 placeholder; per specialty, profession and service-location override; benchmark guardrail at 50% of lowest competitor.
**ADR008** Accepted · Commercial. Why: Makes Kiorly 'free' for providers Kiorex keeps busy — the core wedge against incumbents. Consequence: CR06 settlement engine; informational offset line.
**ADR009** Accepted · Commercial. Why: Zero-risk entry for single practitioners; marketplace supply growth. Consequence: Minimum invoice EGP 100, carry-forward; unpaid → listing paused, Kiorly keeps working.
**ADR010** Accepted · Commercial. Why: Speeds supply acquisition in Egypt while filtering inactive sign-ups. Consequence: 500 clinics; ≥20 checked-in visits in 30 days else 90-day trial.
**ADR011** Accepted · Compliance. Kiorly does no tax-authority ETA integration. Why: Decided after legal review; clinics issue their own receipts. Consequence: FD02 removed fiscal modules; printouts are payment records only.
**ADR012** Accepted · Identity. Why: Do not hand-roll identity; Kiorly launch must not wait. Consequence: Kiorly keeps its auth at launch behind an OIDC-ready seam; providers federate in phase 2.
**ADR013** Accepted · Privacy. Why: Identity roles must not imply chart access. Consequence: Kiorex Account holds subject, credentials, memberships only — no profession, licence or address claims.
**ADR014** Accepted · Platform. Why: New country without code forks; already implemented (Market pack, commercialConfig). Consequence: Market go-live template + certification checklist; packs now also carry the scope-of-practice matrix and district lists for coverage zones.
**ADR015** Accepted · Infrastructure. Why: No in-country hyperscaler region; recorded government approval path (SC01). Consequence: platform/data-cells.json; optional future cell when available.
**ADR016** Accepted · AI. Why: Speed to launch with redaction and consent; cost and control later. Consequence: Provider abstraction; market-specific redaction; plan-gated quotas.
**ADR017** Accepted · Sequencing. Why: Supply and compliance first; marketplace consumes their APIs. Consequence: Pharmacy pilot M13, Lab pilot M19; marketplace verticals follow GA. Kiorly Nurse follows the same pattern (software pilot before Kiorex home-nursing category).
**ADR018** Accepted · Distribution. Why: Apple 5.1.1(ix) requires a legal entity for health apps; multiple accounts add no value. Consequence: Kiorex app + Kiorly app (edition-aware for Kiorly Nurse); field apps only on demand.
**ADR019** Accepted · Brand. Why: Session-cookie isolation and focused SEO. Consequence: Marketing in paths, apps on subdomains (incl. nurse.kiorly.com); kiorly.link for booking links.
**ADR020** Accepted · Trust. Why: Verified-only reviews are a differentiator and a moderation safeguard. Consequence: REVIEW_INVITATION_ELIGIBLE event on COMPLETED (KX08).
**ADR021** Proposed · Commercial. Why: Adds revenue without compromising trust. Consequence: Launch after marketplace liquidity; ranking explanation page.
**ADR022** Proposed · Finance. Why: Seed lasts to M27 in base case; raise from strength. Consequence: Gates in KX13 §10; raise window M15–M20.
**ADR023** Accepted · Platform. Why: Founder decision 19 Nov 2026 — build every module, release it ON, and keep operational control centrally without deploys. Consequence: Flag control plane in kiorex-platform; global · market · organization scopes; any OFF wins; universal OFF contract; Kiorly FF01…06.
**ADR024** Accepted · Compliance. Why: Keeps the default-ON model while preventing regulated features from going live before approval. Consequence: Pharmacy orders, lab bookings, telemedicine, AI navigation, AI receptionist, specialty templates, new markets — and from v2.3 home visits (GHV1/GHV2), physiotherapy category (GPR1), nursing care plans and MAR (GKN1), individual nurse listing (GKN2).
**ADR025** Accepted · Clinical safety. Why: A switch that can disable Rx checks, QC or audit is itself a hazard. Consequence: Fixed list in KX08; CI rejects flag keys on safety paths. v2.3 adds scope-of-practice enforcement, profession verification, home-visit safety timer/SOS/unsafe-abort, address masking and MAR checks.
**ADR026** Accepted · Engineering. Why: Assessment (KX22): kiorex-backend is an untested TypeORM microservice prototype with Stripe/Twilio; kiorex-web-app is UI with dummy data; kiorex-ai-services is empty; backend-documentation describes an unbuilt architecture. Consequence: Build kiorex-platform from the Kiorly skeleton; harvest patient UI only; secret-scan then archive all four repos.
**ADR027** Accepted · Engineering. Why: Keeps repos independently deployable while reusing the kernel and contracts. Consequence: @kiorly/commerce-kernel, @kiorly/field-visit, @kiorex/contracts; semver; 90-day dual support on majors.
**ADR028** Accepted · Infrastructure. Why: Founder decision 19 Nov 2026 — stay on AWS but be able to switch cloud without a rewrite; OpenTofu is open source (MPL 2.0) and free. Consequence: CDK retired before the Kiorly pilot; encrypted S3 state; app adapters for storage, secrets, email; no AWS SDK outside adapters (KX21 §01).
**ADR029 · Profession is a first-class attribute of every clinician** Accepted · Clinical / Data. Why: Physiotherapists and nurses are licensed under different laws and syndicates from doctors (physiotherapy: Law 3 of 1985 [extraction garbled]) and may not do the same things — they must never issue a drug prescription. Today every clinician is implicitly a doctor. Decision: Clinician.profession ∈ PHYSICIAN, DENTIST, PHYSIOTHERAPIST, NURSE, extensible by market pack; a scope-of-practice matrix maps profession × capability. Consequence: @RequiresScope guard (never a flag); verification per profession (PRVER); Kiorex care-type categories; existing clinicians migrate to PHYSICIAN/DENTIST. Rollback: none needed — additive.
**ADR030 · Service location is set per visit type** Accepted · Product / Data. Why: Doctors, physiotherapists and nurses all do home visits; building it per profession would triple the work and split the slot engine. Options: separate home-care product vs. a property of the visit type — the latter chosen. Decision: VisitType.serviceLocation ∈ IN_CLINIC, HOME (VIDEO reserved). Consequence: Slot engine gains coverage zones, travel buffers and home caps; Kiorly remains system of record (ADR005); field logic published as @kiorly/field-visit and reused by Kiorly Lab home collection; home-visit safety controls are never flags; launch-locked behind GHV1/GHV2.
**ADR031 · Kiorly Nurse is an edition of Kiorly Clinic** Accepted · Architecture / Brand. Why: Most of what a nurse or agency needs (Estimated ≈80%) already exists in Kiorly Clinic. A separate product would fork scheduling, payments, messaging and the Kiorex connector. Decision: Organization.edition ∈ CLINIC, NURSE; the NURSE edition has its own name, navigation, terminology, plans and modules (care plans, nursing records, vitals, MAR, shifts, dispatch) and hides doctor-only modules. Consequence: Same repository, API, database, kernel and connector; edition set at sign-up, changed only by the super admin; PRD is KX23.
**ADR032 · Individual nurses and agencies are both tenants** Accepted · Commercial / Compliance. Why: Home nursing in Egypt is delivered by companies and by freelance nurses; excluding individuals leaves most supply out, but listing them to patients without a legal basis is a safety and regulatory risk. Decision: A solo nurse is an organization of one (Nurse Lite / Nurse Pro); agencies use Nurse Agency. Software use is open to both from day one; Kiorex listing of individual nurses is launch-locked (GKN2). Consequence: nurse.individual_kiorex_listing flag; stricter solo verification (ID selfie match, criminal-record certificate — Q8); agencies listed after GHV1/GHV2.

**ADR033 · One Kiorly staff app, tiered mobile entitlements, safety never paywalled** Proposed (drafted 20 Nov 2026, `_docs/mobile/ADR-033_One_Kiorly_App_Tiered_Mobile.md`; becomes Accepted when the founder signs it). Amends the consequence of ADR018 (one store account per platform stays; the Kiorly app is the staff app and hosts the Clinic, Pharmacy and Lab modules and the Nurse edition) and extends ADR025. Decision: one Expo app for provider staff; modules appear only from memberships, roles and per-organization entitlements; entitlements (who may use) are separate from flags (whether a capability exists), both evaluated server-side; safety capabilities (critical lab value, controlled-drug discrepancy, nurse SOS and missed safety check, interaction and allergy warnings, recalls, cold chain) reach every plan including Lite, with no flag and no paid entitlement, guarded by a registry and a CI test; no commerce on iOS (neutral locked text, no prices); device-bound sessions and app lock; offline queue with per-action conflict policy, clinical sign-offs never queued; split into branded builds stays an option by build configuration only. **What is built (23 Nov 2026):** the app ships as **Kiorly** (named Kiorly Work until 22 Nov, Kiorly Team on 22 to 23 Nov; final name Kiorly from 23 Nov, ADR037; repo `kiorly`; bundle id `com.kiorly.app`, scheme `kiorly`) with sign-in entries Clinic, Nurse, Pharmacy and Lab, gated today by a single `mobile_staff_app` entitlement (ON for Pro and Enterprise plans, OFF for Basic/Lite) plus role-based modules from the server; a Basic organization gets a real slice (safety-alert inbox, remote sign-out, upgrade request) and `FEATURE_DISABLED` on every other `/mobile/*` route. The tiered `mobile.*` keys, the safety registry CI test, multi-organization switching and the Passport public client `kiorly-app` are **Planned**, not built. **Name:** final name is plain **Kiorly** (ADR037, 23 Nov 2026), which supersedes the naming in this ADR's earlier text and the interim name Kiorly Team; the tiering decision itself still awaits sign-off (open question Q14).
**ADR034 · The notification layer is a package, not a service** Accepted (founder decision recorded 23 Nov 2026 in `kiorex-contracts/docs/NOTIFICATION_LAYER.md`). Why: a central notification service would make every Kiorly product depend on Kiorex being up (violates principle P1), would carry PHI-adjacent data across products, and adds a service, a database and an on-call rota; the Kiorly pipeline already exists per product. Decision: intent, template registry, PHI guard, routing and fallback, consent and quiet-hours gate, status model and audit shape ship as `@kiorex/contracts/notify` (contracts 1.7.0, pure TypeScript, no I/O); each product injects its own channel adapters, preference store, audit sink, dispatch store and clock. Consequence: migration is one repo at a time (platform done; then Passport, Lab, Pharmacy, Clinic); a central service may be reconsidered only if cross-product operations justify it; Super Admin runtime template and routing control is a Proposed later layer (KX24 §10). Supersedes nothing; clarifies the "no service" row in §04. The Drive draft of KX24 (22 Nov) numbered this decision ADR-033; it is renumbered here because ADR-033 already exists.
**ADR035 · One provider adapter contract for every outbound dependency** Proposed (implemented in kiorex-platform, kiorex-account, clinics-management, kiorly-pharmacy and kiorly-lab; awaiting founder sign-off). Why: founder decision 19 Nov 2026 to run every provider on mock adapters until a go-live pass, and to make going live a configuration change. Decision: each kind (SMS, EMAIL, WHATSAPP, PAYMENT, PUSH, STORAGE, CONSENT, AI) has a port with a `mock` and a `real` implementation selected by `<KIND>_MODE`, an env contract `<KIND>_API_URL`, `<KIND>_API_KEY`, `<KIND>_WEBHOOK_SECRET`, timeouts, retries with backoff on network, timeout, 429 and 5xx (never 4xx) with the same `Idempotency-Key` on every attempt, error mapping without vendor body or recipient, HMAC-SHA256 webhooks over the raw body with 5 min tolerance and de-duplication, forward-only statuses, startup refusal when `MODE=real` lacks a variable (names only), dev-only simulators that answer 404 in production, and one mapping file per kind as the only vendor-specific code. Consequence: only vendor URLs, credentials, template approvals and wire-format mapping remain for go-live; see `_docs/PROVIDER_GO_LIVE.md` and KX24 §05. Open: the consent registry wire format is an assumption; push has no HTTP provider in the clinic and platform repos.
**ADR037 · The staff app is named Kiorly** Accepted (founder decision 23 Nov 2026; App Store name checked). Supersedes the naming in ADR033 and the interim names Kiorly Work and Kiorly Team; the tiering and safety decisions of ADR033 are untouched. Why: three names in four days hurt the store listing and the team's vocabulary; the Kiorly name is the provider brand and the staff app is its mobile home. Decision: display name `Kiorly`; store title `Kiorly: Clinic, Pharmacy & Lab`; "by Kiorex" appears only on the splash screen and in About, never in the icon label or the store name; icon is the Kiorex mark with the Kiorly accent (not a product colour); modules inside are Clinic, Nurse, Pharmacy and Lab; the web products keep their `Kiorly Clinic by Kiorex` lockups; bundle id `com.kiorly.app`, scheme `kiorly`, header `X-Kiorly-Client: kiorly/<version>` (legacy `kiorly-team/*` and `kiorly-work/*` still accepted by all backends); Arabic name كيورلي. Consequence: docs, brand notes and PDFs use the new name; the repo folder `kiorly-team` is renamed to `kiorly` by the lead and the code and config rename is done by other agents; Apple and Google organization registration and the Passport public client stay NEEDS_HUMAN (the ids above are placeholders until registered). Rollback: renaming is cheap before store registration and costly after.
**ADR038 · Kiorly Home: one web sign-in for the Kiorly products** Accepted (founder decision, 23 Nov 2026; mirrors the Kiorly mobile app of ADR-037; AMENDED 24 Nov 2026: Home now has parity with the mobile sign-in screen, see (1), (3) and (9); the earlier "sign in first, then pick a workspace" picker is retired). Why: provider staff use Clinic, Nurse, Pharmacy and Lab, and today each web product has its own sign-in; the mobile app already presents one sign-in screen. Decision: (1) A static Vite React app, Kiorly Home (`kiorex-account/apps/kiorly-home`), is the sign-in entry at `https://www.kiorly.com/signin` (kiorly.com redirects to www). It has the same screen as the Kiorly mobile app: a header with the Kiorly lockup, the AR/EN toggle and the line "One sign-in for Clinic, Nurse, Pharmacy and Lab"; a card "Sign in" / "Choose your workplace product" with four tiles, Clinic, Nurse (an EDITION of Clinic: edition NURSE, host nurse.kiorly.com), Pharmacy and Lab (same icons and product accents as the app: Teal #0D9488, Rose, Green #16A34A, Violet #7C3AED); the last tile is remembered on the browser, a deep link (`/signin?to=pharmacy`, `/signin/lab`) preselects a tile, `?switch=1` lands on the same screen. Then exactly TWO methods, Kiorex Passport (DEFAULT, preselected, primary button "Sign in with Kiorex Passport") and Password (the last method that signed in is remembered). Light is the default theme with a persisted Light / Dark / System choice. (2) Method Passport: Home is the public PKCE client `kiorly-home`; after the code exchange Home opens the CHOSEN product at `<product>/login?sso=passport&from=kiorly-home[&edition=NURSE]` and the product completes its OWN silent Passport code flow (the person types the code once). Home asks Passport for the person's workspaces only to show a gentle hint: when the person has exactly one workspace and it is not the chosen tile, Home offers both ("Open <your product>" or "Continue to <chosen>"); it never blocks. No enumeration: Home never asks for a phone number or e-mail itself and calls no workspace API before the IdP has verified the code. (3) Method Password is a single-use HANDOFF so Home never touches a product token: Home's form posts the e-mail and password (Lab: organisation slug and e-mail, it has no passwords) to the chosen product API `POST /auth/handoff/login` (CORS and an Origin allow-list for the Home origin only, `KIORLY_HOME_ORIGINS`, derived from `config/domains.json`). The API applies the same credential checks, throttling and audit as its normal login and answers with an opaque single-use code (256 random bits, stored only as a SHA-256 hash, 30-second lifetime, bound to that product (and edition) and to the Home origin) plus the redirect URL, never a token. Home navigates to `<product>/login?handoff=<code>[&edition=NURSE]`; the product web strips the code from the address bar and calls `POST /auth/handoff/exchange`, which returns the normal login result once; a replay, an expired or foreign code is a uniform 401, and the code is never logged. When the handoff cannot complete (second factor, expired password, organisation choice) the API answers `status: FALLBACK` with a `reason` and Home sends the person to the product's own login with `?reason=<flag>`, which shows a notice and the product's own form: it never fails silently. (4) Patients are not part of it (they stay on Kiorex). (5) Products stay separate deployments with their own URLs and keep working on their own, without Kiorex: no shared database and no source imports between products; Home only calls the two handoff endpoints each product API exposes and redirects. (6) The workspaces endpoint on Passport (`GET /api/workspaces`, derived from organisation memberships) stays, used only for the hint in (2); the "Create or join" screen is replaced by a "New to this product? Create a <product> workspace" link to the product's existing sign-up (Clinic and Pharmacy `/signup`, Nurse `/signup?edition=nurse`, Lab `/onboarding`). (7) Sign-out from Home ends the Passport session by RP-initiated logout with `id_token_hint` and returns to Home; products register Home's `/signin` as an allowed post-logout URI. (8) Domains are configuration only: `kiorex-account/config/domains.json` is the single table per environment (local, staging, production, including the product API hosts); the Passport client registrations, the Home build, the products' `KIORLY_HOME_ORIGINS` and CORS lists, app-link files and the OpenTofu module `kiorly-domains` derive from or are asserted against it by tests that fail if a production entry contains localhost or an unlisted host. Production hosts: www.kiorly.com (Home), clinic.kiorly.com (app.kiorly.com stays an alias), nurse.kiorly.com, pharmacy.kiorly.com, lab.kiorly.com, APIs api.clinic / api.pharmacy / api.lab .kiorly.com; Passport stays on account.kiorex.com. (9) Password sign-in is offered only where the product API has a password sign-in (AUTH_MODE local or dev); a production deployment that is Passport-only answers 404 `LOCAL_LOGIN_DISABLED` and Home explains it and switches to Passport. Consequence: one more small Passport client, one more static site, one small table and two endpoints per product API. Not verified: any deploy, DNS, certificates, the production Passport client, the production `KIORLY_HOME_ORIGINS` values and the CDN/WAF rules for the new endpoints (NEEDS_HUMAN: Docs/NEEDS_HUMAN.md in kiorex-account); Clinic has no password-expiry or organisation-picker step today, so only the second-factor fallback is exercised. SECOND AMENDMENT, 24 Nov 2026 (founder approved): per-organisation default sign-in. Every Kiorly product web login (Clinic incl. the Nurse edition, Pharmacy, Lab) now has the Kiorly Home design language for ONE product (split screen with a branded panel, centred card, Passport / Password methods, Sun/Moon/Monitor theme icon and globe language control, light default, AR/EN RTL) and a "Switch product" link to Kiorly Home (`<home>/signin?switch=1`, from the domain config, hidden when Home is not configured so standalone deployments never depend on it). Both methods are always offered when Passport is available; the new organisation setting `defaultSignInMethod` (PASSWORD | PASSPORT | automatic) only chooses which one is PRESELECTED. Stored per organisation in each product API (own column, migration, audit entry `settings.default_signin_method`, owner/admin roles only, AR/EN admin UI). Automatic = PASSPORT for an organisation linked to Kiorex (marketplace link or Passport-linked staff), PASSWORD otherwise; an owner override always wins. ## ADR-039 Telehealth over our own peer-to-peer WebRTC (Accepted, 23 Nov 2026)
Source of record: `clinics-management/Docs/adr/ADR-039-telehealth-p2p-webrtc.md` (status "accepted by the founder for implementation"). Plan: `_docs/telehealth/TELEHEALTH_PLAN.md`; Kiorex side: `_docs/CHANGES_2026-11-23_telehealth_p2p_kiorex_side.md`.
**Context.** Founder decision: video-call cost must be the minimum. **Decision.** (1) The Clinic owns the telehealth session (`TelehealthSession` 1:1 with an appointment of service location VIDEO); the Clinic stays the only system of record for appointments (ADR-005). (2) Media is browser-to-browser WebRTC with the native `RTCPeerConnection`; no media passes our servers, no recording, no paid video vendor (not Daily, Twilio, Chime or LiveKit). (3) Signaling is HTTP polling (about 1.2 s) behind a `SignalingTransport` interface; SDP and ICE only, no PHI, rows purged after 24 h. (4) ICE: Cloudflare STUN plus Cloudflare Realtime TURN with credentials minted per join; with no key the join returns STUN-only. (5) The join window is computed from the appointment at join time (opens 10 minutes before the start, closes 60 minutes after the end) and is never stored in the link. (6) The patient link token is an HMAC of session, patient and link version; regenerating the link revokes older tokens. (7) Attendance equals check-in (both present 3 minutes) and runs the existing commission path, so EGP 40 per attended new Kiorex patient bills once. (8) Flag `telehealth.video` (Module, ON by default) and entitlement `telehealth_video`. **Consequences.** Kiorex stops creating Daily rooms (the Daily adapter and its env keys were removed; `VIDEO_MODE=mock|clinic-p2p`), stores `BookingIntent.telehealth` from the Clinic events and shows the card in web and the patient app (new native module `react-native-webview`, native rebuild needed). The contract schemas for the telehealth block are not published in `@kiorex/contracts` (not in 1.11.0). **Not verified:** a real call on devices over TURN, the Cloudflare TURN key (NEEDS_HUMAN, owned by the Clinic), video consent wording and the clinical-governance gate for `kiorex.telemedicine`. **Tests:** Clinic `telehealth*.test.ts` including 7 edge tests; Kiorex `test/telehealth.int.spec.ts`.

## ADR-040 Connecting standalone organisations to Kiorex (Accepted, 24 Nov 2026)
**Context.** Every Kiorly product works without Kiorex (ADR-001/002). Clinics, nurses, pharmacies and labs that started standalone must be able to join the marketplace later, guided, and Kiorex ops must verify them with as little manual work as possible while every human decision stays human.

**Decision.**
1. **Product side: "Connect to Kiorex" wizard** (Clinic and Nurse edition on branch `kiorex`; Pharmacy; Lab). The API computes a step checklist from real state, each step `done | action_needed | waiting_kiorex | blocked`, with a deep link to the screen that fixes it. Clinic/Nurse: subscription, entitlement (`clinic.kiorex_listing`), a VERIFIED doctor, a bookable visit type with a fee, availability with slots, profile completeness (80 %), consent to the marketplace terms and the fee acknowledgement (the wording reads the published market fee schedule, never a hard-coded price), optional telehealth/home visits, then Request to go live. Pharmacy/Lab: branch opt-in or lab site, priced catalogue, hours/collection, licence number and document, payout LABEL placeholder (account-number-like input is refused, no bank data is stored), terms, request registration.
2. **A standalone organisation never lists on its own.** Self-serve sign-up writes `Tenant.providerSettings.kiorexConnect = {status: STANDALONE}`; the listing eligibility function gains a last-position `connection` blocker. `Request to go live` moves listings DRAFT -> PENDING_VERIFICATION; they become LIVE automatically when Kiorex ops approves and every other gate is green. Organisations without that document (existing and imported ones) are legacy-connected and never gated. Pharmacy (`settings.mode`) and Lab (`integrationMode`) already have STANDALONE/INTEGRATED and keep it: a request switches them to INTEGRATED, a rejection switches them back.
3. **Signed, PHI-free request.** `POST /api/v1/partners/v1/connection-requests` on Kiorex, idempotent per `(kind, orgRef)`, signed `sha256=HMAC(secret, ts.rawBody)`: Clinic/Nurse with the existing Kiorly webhook secret; Pharmacy/Lab with the webhook secret they submit (integrity only: control of the partner API is proven by the signed handshake). The body holds counts, booleans and credentials, never patient data. Pharmacy/Lab submit a freshly issued HMAC client + webhook secret in the same request; Kiorex stores them AES-GCM encrypted and, at Go live, moves them into the existing partner registry and wipes the request copy. Secrets are shown once (rotation response) and never logged. The partner polls its status with a status token it derives itself (HMAC of its org id), so no token is stored.
4. **Ops console: Partner onboarding.** A queue and a per-partner wizard. AUTOMATED steps run on intake and on "Run automated checks again" (idempotent): credentials, signed handshake against the partner API, catalogue/provider dry run counts, flag state (including launch-gate evidence), market/pack, duplicate detection, required documents and terms. HUMAN steps are highlighted: review documents, verify licence, confirm fees (organisation-scope policy override only through the existing Policy page), launch-gate evidence for `kiorex.pharmacy_orders` and `kiorex.lab_bookings` (recorded ONLY through the existing human super-admin route, never by this module), approval (two-person rule: the approver is a super admin and is neither the requester nor the licence verifier), Go live. Go live opens only when every step is green and is idempotent; it activates the registry entry, ensures the partner's org-scope flags are ON and tells Clinic over the signed marketplace API. Pause/resume, request changes and reject need a reason. Every action is audited without PHI and notifies the partner through the mock notification layer.
5. **After Go live** the wizard offers to make Kiorex Passport the organisation's default sign-in (the `defaultSignInMethod` setting of ADR-038) and to invite staff to link their phone.

**Consequences.** No new contract version: the intake is additive and lives outside the published OpenAPI files (follow-up: publish it in kiorex-contracts). The partner registry still routes outbound calls to the first active registration of a kind, so several simultaneously live pharmacies or labs need per-order routing (follow-up, unchanged by this ADR). Production still needs real notification providers, counsel-approved terms and fee wording (NEEDS_HUMAN) and the real launch-gate evidence.

## Growth Engine decisions (24 Nov 2026): ADR-041 to ADR-048 (Proposed)
Status line: **all seven are Proposed and unsigned.** Nothing here binds code or documents until the founder signs. Source: `_docs/growth/GROWTH_ARCHITECTURE_DECISIONS.md` section 23. Numbering note (Rev 2.4): ADR-039 exists (telehealth, Accepted, above), so the Growth decisions keep ADR-041 to ADR-047 and Q18 is closed. Implementation status of each is stated in its paragraph; none is signed. ADR-048 (two separate credit systems and provider-sponsored promotions, Proposed, founder-approved design of 24 Nov 2026) is recorded below; the WhatsApp assistant tool architecture decision that was reserved as ADR-048 and never written is ADR-051 (Rev 2.7, scheme A; the founder confirms the numbering, launch guide 11.1). Detail: KX-27 to KX-33.

**ADR041** Proposed · Architecture. Why: Growth must reuse the two modular monoliths and the Postgres outbox; a new service, Redis or Kafka adds cost and a second security surface for volumes that do not need it. Decision: growth is a module family in the Kiorex API and the Kiorly Clinic API; Kiorly Clinic owns provider-owned assets and the money outcome (links, QR, widget settings, appointment attribution fields, commission decision); Kiorex owns demand-side evidence (touchpoints, visitor identity under consent, the attribution decision, SEO, campaigns, referral, rewards, lifecycle, analytics); they exchange one signed block on the existing booking call. Consequence: no new deployables; cross-product data is additive (contracts 1.10.0); growth cannot work in a standalone Kiorly org beyond links, QR and widget settings. Supersedes: none. Extends ADR-004, ADR-005, ADR-006. **Implementation (Rev 2.4):** built that way: no new service, Redis or Kafka; growth modules in the two monoliths (`growth/*`, `public-site/*`, clinic `growth/*`). Still unsigned.

**ADR042** Proposed (money: needs a founder decision) · Commercial. Why: the provider promise "no fee for patients you bring" is half built; the evidence upstream of the booking does not exist and `attributionJson` is free-form. Decision: attribution is a deterministic pure function of (booking, touchpoint evidence, versioned policy) producing an immutable `AttributionDecision` with reason codes and an inputs hash; origin is `PROVIDER_LED`, `MARKETPLACE` or `UNATTRIBUTED`; default precedence `PROVIDER_FIRST`; the clinic writes an append-only `KiorexCommissionDecision` for charge and no-charge; `BookingSource` values are not added or renamed; evidence can remove a fee and never add one. Consequence: a new dispute reason, contracts 1.10.0, public rules for providers; any rule charging more than today is out of scope and needs the CEO. Extends ADR-007. **Implementation (Rev 2.4):** built locally with the proposed defaults (`PROVIDER_FIRST`, 30/30/7 days, no charge for provider-led) marked `PROPOSED_DEFAULT`; the CEO has not signed the money decision.

**ADR043** Proposed · Architecture / SEO. Why: the patient web is a client-only SPA that crawlers and link-preview bots cannot read, which contradicts the KX-14 promise of indexed provider profiles. Decision: hybrid server rendering for public routes only, as a Vite SSR bundle served by the Kiorex API image in `ROLE=site` and cached by CloudFront; canonical URL `https://kiorex.com/{locale}/{cc}/{type}/{slug}` with ASCII slugs identical in AR and EN, slug history and 301 from legacy ids; thin-page guard; no Next.js migration. Consequence: one more deploy role, a CDN behaviour set, a flag fallback to the SPA shell. Extends ADR-019. **Implementation (Rev 2.4):** built locally (SSR roles of the API image, slugs, guard, sitemaps, JSON-LD); not deployed, no Lighthouse data.

**ADR044** Proposed · Security / Architecture. Why: every host sends `frame-ancestors 'none'` (ADR-038 statement) but provider websites need an embeddable booking widget. Decision: widgets run on a dedicated origin (proposed `book.kiorex.com`, host name Open) whose `frame-ancestors` is generated per request from an exact-origin allowlist (https, no wildcard, at most 10); every other host keeps `'none'`; widget bookings use the same hold and idempotent confirm flow and carry a signed `lt` token. Consequence: an explicit, narrow amendment of the ADR-038 sentence "nothing embeds another product". Amends ADR-038. **Implementation (Rev 2.4):** built locally (widget host role, per-request CSP, SDK with SRI); host name and DNS still open; other hosts keep `frame-ancestors 'none'`.

**ADR045** Proposed (needs counsel) · Privacy / Analytics. Why: the existing analytics ingest is deliberately anonymous and cannot attribute or build funnels. Decision: server-side operational events always allowed; a pseudonymous `visitorKey` only after consent (or the signed in-session `lt` token as contractual necessity); specialty and provider never join the visitor stream; reports use k-anonymity with k at least 10; the existing anonymous endpoint is unchanged; ad platforms receive only `AnonymizedConversion`; no health-condition targeting, no patient-list audiences, no remarketing. Consequence: a deliberate change of the "no identifier" rule for a new, consent-gated table; legal bases are Regulatory dependencies. **Implementation (Rev 2.4):** built locally with `growth.analytics_visitor` launch-locked until counsel; the visitor key is therefore not issued in any environment.

**ADR046** Proposed (blocked on legal assessment) · Commercial / Compliance. Why: patient referral can lower CAC but a reward balance may be regulated as stored value. Decision: if cleared, rewards are closed-loop, non-transferable, non-cashable discount credit in a new append-only ledger (a fourth ledger, never commingled with Kiorex Pay, held deposits or SaaS credit), issued only after the referee's attended first visit and refund window, with a coupon-code fallback and no balance object if counsel says a wallet is regulated. Consequence: Phase 2 build waits for counsel and finance treatment. **Implementation (Rev 2.4):** not built (Roadmap); blocked on the legal assessment.

**ADR047** Proposed · Governance. Why: `PolicySetting` has one row per key, no effective date and no maker-checker, so attribution windows and reward rules cannot be reproduced. Decision: growth configuration is versioned data (`GrowthConfigVersion`: immutable versions, scope, effective dates, two-person approval, rollback by new version, secrets by reference only, money-policy kinds effective at least 7 days after approval); feature switches stay in the flags control plane. Consequence: decisions cite the policy version in force; ops gains a configuration page. **Implementation (Rev 2.4):** built locally (`GrowthConfigService`, five editable kinds, maker-checker also as a database CHECK).

**ADR048** Proposed (needs counsel for the patient CREDIT mode; accounting for sponsored discounts) · Commercial / Compliance. Status date 24 Nov 2026, founder-approved product design, not yet signed. Title: **Two separate credit systems and provider-sponsored promotions.** Why: growth needs rewards, service recovery and a refund option for patients, and a way for providers to offset Kiorex fees and to fund their own patient discounts, but a general-purpose wallet would be a regulated stored-value product, would invite arbitrage between patients and providers, and would mix patients' money with providers' credit. Decision: (1) **Two separate credit systems, one experience.** *Patient credit* (Kiorex, closed loop, spendable only on Kiorex bookings and Kiorex-collected amounts) and *provider credit* (Kiorly, the existing CR-05 wallet: one wallet per tenant, append-only ledger, grant, first-expiring-first consume, expire, late-reversal credit, daily reconcile). Separate ledgers, tables, services and repositories; **no conversion in either direction**, no shared balance row, no cross-ledger entries; they share only the ledger pattern. The Kiorex app shows patient credit, Kiorly shows provider credit. Not a general-purpose money wallet. (2) **Patient credit** comes from referral and welcome rewards (after a friend's first completed visit or one's own), goodwill and service-recovery grants by support (provider late cancellation, long wait, failed booking), refund credit and campaign credit with expiry and service or specialty scope. **At refund time the patient chooses "refund to original payment method" (the default selection) or "keep as credit"; credit is an option, never forced.** Credit applies to a booking deposit or prepayment before the card, each line shown at checkout, order credit, deposit, card. Family accounts share credit across dependants with one owner. Safety rules: one-way and non-cash (no withdrawal, no peer-to-peer transfer, no cash conversion), expiry and per-user and per-campaign caps on every grant, issuance only on verified qualifying events (completed visits, never sign-up or an unfinished booking), reversal when the qualifying booking is refunded or cancelled, never counted as Kiorex revenue, patient top-ups and loads not allowed, no paying outside Kiorex. **Patient CREDIT mode stays launch-locked and counsel-gated; coupon mode is the default.** (3) **Provider credit** pays Kiorex fees (commission and subscription draw from credit first, then the invoice), carries goodwill, waivers and dispute-win returns, onboarding, free-month, setup and connect credit, provider referral rewards, usage purchases (messaging, SMS and WhatsApp bundles, storage packs) and marketing spend if added; it is never patients' money (patient deposits stay in the held ledger until the visit), has no provider-to-provider transfer and no cash-out of promotional credit. (4) **Providers may fund patient discounts from their own credit, as a provider-sponsored promotion, never as a conversion of balances:** the clinic configures a promotion (scope, discount, budget cap, expiry, eligibility); the discount shows at checkout as a sponsored line; a budget reservation is held at booking; on visit completion the amount is debited from the clinic wallet (or invoiced if insufficient, per configuration) as a promotion expense; cancel and refund release or reverse it; full audit; it appears in clinic statements and in the Kiorex booking ledger as a distinct provider-sponsored discount line; **commission is computed on the fee before the sponsored discount unless the versioned policy `SPONSORED_PROMOTION_POLICY` says otherwise**; advertising-compliance review (no inducement for clinical decisions) and maker-checker for high-value promotions in Super Admin. Consequence: two ledger implementations to keep honest with shared vectors, new flags (all launch-locked except the provider wallet extension), contracts 1.12.0 additive fields (`sponsoredDiscount`, `feeBasisMinor`, `tender`), counsel and accounting items (launch guide 11.24 to 11.31), packages `GRW-P2-B2` (rebuilt), `GRW-P2-W` and `GRW-P2-S`. Refines ADR-046 (which described one patient reward ledger); extends ADR-007 (fee unchanged) and ADR-041 (promotions are provider-owned assets). **Implementation (24 Nov 2026):** only the Clinic CR-05 wallet exists; nothing else is built. Open: maximum open balance, refund-credit expiry behaviour (proposed: remainder returns to the original method), order-of-use interpretation, high-value thresholds.
