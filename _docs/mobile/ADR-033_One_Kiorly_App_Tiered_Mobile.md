# ADR-033 · One Kiorly app with Clinic, Pharmacy and Lab modules, tiered mobile entitlements, safety never paywalled

**Proposed** · Distribution / Commercial / Clinical safety · drafted 20 Nov 2026 · records the founder's decisions of 20 Nov 2026; becomes **Accepted** when the founder signs it off.
**Amends** ADR-018 (consequence only). **Extends** ADR-025 (adds a never-paywalled rule). **Relates to** ADR-023, ADR-024, ADR-027, ADR-031, ADR-032, KX-19 §04, KX-12 principle P5.
**Supersedes** the in-repo design of the app repo (then `kiorly-work`, now `kiorly`) (single `mobile_staff_app` entitlement, upgrade screen). **Naming:** the final app name is plain **Kiorly** (ADR-037, 23 Nov 2026), which supersedes the naming in this ADR's amendments of 23 Nov (the interim "Kiorly Team").

*Format follows `_docs/rev23/KX-01_Decision_Log_ADRs.md` (title line, Why, Consequence), expanded with Context and Decision because this ADR changes two earlier ones.*

## Context
* ADR-018 (Accepted): "One store account per platform; apps by audience." Consequence as recorded in Rev 2.3: "Kiorex app + Kiorly app (edition-aware for Kiorly Nurse); field apps only on demand." The sentence "no separate Kiorly Nurse app" lives in KX-19 §04 and `CHANGES_2.2_to_2.3.md` item 294, not in the ADR text itself.
* KX-19 lists the Kiorly app audience as doctors, physiotherapists, nurses and clinic staff, says "PWA remains primary at launch", and names possible later field apps "Kiorly Lab Collect" and "Kiorly Pharmacy Rider".
* A first mobile build (named Kiorly Work, then Kiorly Team) was created with one `mobile_staff_app` entitlement that excludes Lite/entry plans, an iOS upgrade screen listing Pro features, and per-product login. It conflicts with the founder's product decisions and with store policy for iOS.
* ADR-025 makes safety controls never feature flags (fixed list in KX-08 §09; CI rejects flag keys on safety paths). It does not itself forbid putting a safety control behind a *plan*. The nearest precedent that does is Rev 2.3 principle P5 / KX-12 / KX-23: "home-visit safety is in every plan".

## Decision
1. **One Expo app, named "Kiorly"** (final, founder decision 23 Nov 2026, ADR-037; store title `Kiorly: Clinic, Pharmacy & Lab`; "by Kiorex" only on splash and About), for provider staff. It hosts three product modules: **Clinic** (re-branded as the Nurse edition when the active organization is a Nurse tenant, ADR-031), **Pharmacy** and **Lab**. Product names stay singular. The name "Kiorly" is final; "Kiorly Team" and "Kiorly Work" are retired.
2. **Modules come from the server**: a module appears only from memberships + roles + per-organization entitlements. A person may belong to several organizations; switching organization re-evaluates entitlements at once; entitlements are never cached across organizations.
3. **Tiered mobile entitlements per organization per product**: `mobile.clinic.core|pro`, `mobile.pharmacy.core|pro`, `mobile.lab.core|pro`, plus `mobile.safety` and an enterprise-controls key. Default mapping: Lite/entry = core + safety; Pro = core + pro + safety; Clinic plan = as Pro; Enterprise = as Pro + enterprise controls (enforced biometrics, session policy, MDM-friendly config). Chain editions map as Pro-equivalent. Configurable per market and overridable per organization.
4. **Feature flags and entitlements are different questions**: flags (global, default ON, super-admin switchable, ADR-023) decide whether a capability *exists*; entitlements decide which organizations *may use* it. `available = flag ON AND entitled`, both evaluated **server-side**; the app only reflects. Existing launch locks (ADR-024) still apply (e.g. `clinic.home_visits`).
5. **Safety is never paywalled (extension of ADR-025).** The following reach **every plan including Lite/entry, in every market, regardless of subscription state**: critical lab values, controlled-drug discrepancies, nurse-safety and panic alerts (SOS, missed safety check, unsafe-abort), drug-interaction and allergy warnings, recalls and cold-chain alerts, and any other patient-safety alert on the signed list. They are modelled as capabilities of kind `safety` that map **only** to the always-granted, non-removable entitlement `mobile.safety`, have no flag, and require nothing else. A static registry plus a CI test in each backend fails if a safety capability is attached to a paid entitlement or a flag, if `mobile.safety` is absent from any plan, or if it can be overridden off. Changing the safety list needs a new ADR and a `kiorex-contracts` release.
6. **Lite gets a real mobile tier** (schedule, check-in, patient summary, notifications, safety alerts, price/stock lookup, critical-value acknowledge, etc.). No empty locked app.
7. **No commerce on iOS.** Locked features show neutral text only ("Not included in your organization's current plan. Your clinic owner can manage plans from the Kiorly web app."): no prices, plan names with prices, upgrade/buy buttons or payment links. Upgrading happens on web or through sales.
8. **Device security**: device-bound sessions with Keychain/Keystore refresh tokens, app lock, encrypted minimal cache with wipe on sign-out/org removal/remote wipe, screenshot protection and blurred app switcher on clinical screens, no patient data in push bodies, mobile access audited like web.
9. **Offline**: queued actions carry idempotency key, client timestamp, device id and user id, with an explicit conflict policy per action type; clinical sign-offs (prescription signing, result release) are online only and never queued.
10. **Split option retained**: the same codebase can later ship branded builds ("Kiorly Clinic", "Kiorly Pharmacy", "Kiorly Lab") by build-time configuration only. Triggers are documented in `MOBILE_APP_DESIGN.md` §11 (store discoverability, release cadence, review risk, app size, enterprise MDM, market regulation).

## Amendment to ADR-018
ADR-018's principle (one store account per platform, apps by audience) is unchanged. Its **consequence** is clarified:
> Kiorex app (patients and families) + **Kiorly app** (provider staff: edition-aware for Kiorly Nurse, and hosting the **Clinic, Pharmacy and Lab** modules). Field apps ("Kiorly Lab Collect", "Kiorly Pharmacy Rider" in KX-19) remain **only on demand**; they are the *split option*, not part of the launch plan.

Consequences for KX-19 §04 (text to update after sign-off, not edited by this ADR): the Kiorly app audience adds pharmacy and lab staff; "PWA remains primary at launch" needs a founder ruling on whether the native app ships beside the PWA (open question Q1); the field-app names must not be reused for the split builds.

## Why
* Pharmacy and lab staff are the same kind of audience as clinic staff (verified staff, org context switching, safety alerts) and one binary means one store review surface, one account, one set of demo logins, one design system.
* Gating the whole app on a Pro plan (the earlier design) excludes Lite organizations, where safety value is highest and conversion evidence is most useful; a paid-only app also tends to look like an upsell wall on iOS.
* Safety capabilities delivered to only some plans would make a panic alert, a critical lab value or a controlled-drug discrepancy a revenue feature. Rev 2.3 already accepted this for home visits (P5); this ADR generalises it so no later pricing change can quietly undo it.
* Separating flags (existence, operations) from entitlements (commerce) keeps the super admin switch semantics of ADR-023 intact and lets support grant or revoke access per organization without touching feature availability.

## Consequences
* **Backends** (clinics-management `kiorex`, kiorly-pharmacy, kiorly-lab): new `mobile.*` entitlement keys and `GET /mobile/me/entitlements?org=`; `@MobileCapability` guard replaces the single-key interceptor; `mobile.safety` forced true in `resolve()`; safety never-paid test in each repo; `kiorex-contracts` gains the capability registry and new flag keys (`mobile.clinic|pharmacy|lab`); `mobile_staff_app` is migrated (core + safety for every org, pro where it was true) and removed after one release.
* **Existing contracts**: web keeps `402 PLAN_UPGRADE_REQUIRED`; mobile clients get `403 ENTITLEMENT_REQUIRED` and `403 FEATURE_DISABLED {key,scope}`; queued actions replayed after a downgrade get `409 ENTITLEMENT_CHANGED` and are retained, never lost.
* **Identity**: Passport public client for the Kiorly app (id `kiorly`, redirect `kiorly://auth/callback`; registration is NEEDS_HUMAN; the earlier proposals `kiorly-app` and `kiorly-team` are superseded by ADR-037); Clinic needs a Passport token exchange and must allow one Passport subject in several Clinic organizations.
* **Repo**: the repo formerly called `kiorly-work` was renamed `kiorly-team` and, on 24 Nov 2026, `kiorly` (founder decisions of 23 Nov 2026; GitHub `mshall/kiorly`, local folder `kiorly/`). Identifiers: bundle id `com.kiorly.app`, scheme `kiorly`, header `kiorly/<v>`. The repo is salvaged per the migration table in the design, and its upgrade screen and `mobile_staff_app` logic deleted. Stores and the Passport client must be registered with the new bundle id (NEEDS_HUMAN); remove old simulator installs of `com.kiorly.work` and `com.kiorly.team`. Backends accept `kiorly/<v>` and the legacy `kiorly-team/<v>` and `kiorly-work/<v>` client headers.
* **Docs to update after sign-off**: `CLAUDE.md` and `INTEGRATION.md` (Kiorly rows), `kiorly/Docs/ENTITLEMENTS.md`, the entitlement sections of the three `Docs/MOBILE.md`, KX-19 §04, KX-01 index (add ADR033, amend ADR018 consequence).
* **Human gates untouched**: store accounts, Apple/Google review, counsel and CMO sign-offs (NH-*), prices and plan composition remain NEEDS_HUMAN; this ADR sets no price.
* **Decision-log index**: the ADR row is **not** added to `_docs/rev23/KX-01_Decision_Log_ADRs.md` (a verbatim transcription of an Approved reference edition, and this ADR is only Proposed). Proposed row once accepted:

## How to change it
A new ADR. Moving a capability **into** the safety list or reducing paid tiers needs only a contracts release plus founder approval; moving a capability **out of** the safety list, or attaching any safety capability to a paid entitlement or a flag, is not allowed by this ADR and needs a superseding ADR signed by the founder and the clinical-safety owner (CMO/DPO per KX-16).
