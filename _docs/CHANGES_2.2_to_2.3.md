## Summary of what changed

1. **New product: Kiorly Nurse (KX-23, new volume, ADR-031).** It is an EDITION of Kiorly Clinic (`Organization.edition = CLINIC | NURSE`), same code/DB/API/commerce kernel/Kiorex connector. **No new repository, no new database** (KX-08, KX-22 explicit NOTE and "Never" rule: do not create a `kiorly-nurse` repo). Domain `nurse.kiorly.com` is an alias of the Clinic stack. Still six target repos. The suite is now "six products" in KX-18 / "five products, two brands" in KX-00.
2. **Four new ADRs (ADR-001..032 now; Accepted 30, Proposed 2).** ADR-029 professions + scope of practice (`Clinician.profession` PHYSICIAN|DENTIST|PHYSIOTHERAPIST|NURSE, `@RequiresScope` guard, 403 `SCOPE_NOT_ALLOWED`); ADR-030 home visits (`VisitType.serviceLocation` IN_CLINIC|HOME, coverage zones, travel buffers, new shared package `@kiorly/field-visit`); ADR-031 Nurse = edition; ADR-032 solo nurse = org of one, individual listing launch-locked (GKN2). New principle P12. Open questions Q7..Q13 added.
3. **New cross-cutting capabilities: professions/physio pack (KX-05 §06-07, EGP 300/org/mo add-on), home visits (KX-05 §08, HV* requirements), Kiorex-side home-visit marketplace (KX-03 KXMHV01-10, KXMPAY07, KXMOP12), identity additions (KX-04 KA13/KA14).** Business rules BR49-BR61 and journeys J6/J7 (KX-02).
4. **New launch gates:** GHV1, GHV2 (home visits), GPR1/GPR2 (physiotherapy), GKN1 (care plans + MAR), GKN2 (individual nurse listing). KX-17 adds G5 (home visits M10-12) and G6 (Nurse pilot M13-15). ADR-024 launch-locked list extended; ADR-025 never-a-flag list extended (scope of practice, profession verification, safety timer/SOS/unsafe-abort, address masking, MAR checks).
5. **Contracts (KX-09 v2.3 minor, additive):** provider-sync fields, `serviceLocation=HOME&district`, `visitAddress` on holds, 422 `OUT_OF_COVERAGE`, `POST /marketplace/v1/care-plan-requests` (P3), events HOME_VISIT_ASSIGNED / _EN_ROUTE / _ABORTED_UNSAFE, SAFETY_ALERT_RAISED, CARE_PLAN_CONFIRMED / _ENDED, ReferralIssued, MedicationOrderShared. Money: home surcharge never enters S or C; unsafe-abort deposit held for review; agency pays its own nurses (Kiorex Pay never pays agency nurses).
6. **Flags:** +15 keys in the catalogue (KX-08 §): clinic.profession.physiotherapy, clinic.home_visits, clinic.home_route_view, clinic.home_dispatch; nurse.edition, nurse.individual_tenants, nurse.care_plans, nurse.mar, nurse.private_duty_shifts, nurse.family_updates, nurse.individual_kiorex_listing; kiorex.home_visits, kiorex.category.physiotherapy, kiorex.category.nursing, kiorex.visit_tracking.
7. **Pricing (all Proposed):** Nurse Lite EGP 0 + Kiorex fees (EGP 100 min invoice); Nurse Pro EGP 399/nurse/month (299 annual-equivalent); Nurse Agency EGP 1,999/month incl. 10 nurses + 149 per extra nurse; Enterprise custom. Kiorex fee stays EGP 40 flat per attended new patient, now overridable per profession and per service location; care-plan follow-ups and unsafe-aborted visits never charged; founding cohort of 200 for Nurse. Clinic ladder prices unchanged (Pro 1,299/999, Clinic 3,499 incl. 5 clinicians, extra 699); "doctor" -> "clinician". Financial model outputs UNCHANGED (physio/home/Nurse not yet modelled; KX-13 §04 lists drivers to add).
8. **Schedule:** Q3 profession model + physio + `@kiorly/field-visit` + Nurse discovery; Q4 home visits (doctors, physios); Q5 Nurse build + pilot (10 solo, 3 agencies); Q6 Nurse GA (M16-18) + Kiorex home-nursing category. Pharmacy/Lab/Series A dates unchanged; if capacity binds, **Nurse GA slips before Pharmacy GA** (R17). New hires: home-care product lead + nursing advisor (M7), 24/7 safety on-call rota (M10). Marketing calendar now 18 months.
9. **Infra:** no change to KX-21 (still Rev 2.2). Implied: DNS/ACM/CloudFront alias for `nurse.kiorly.com` on the Clinic stack, Amazon Location (maps) added in KX-08 C4 context (must sit behind a `MODE=mock` adapter), safety-alert (<=60 s) alarming/on-call, geostamp retention (12 months then district only).
10. **Compliance (KX-15):** physio licensing (Law 3/1985), nursing syndicate membership, agency licensing (Law 51/1981), location data under PDPL; risks R15-R18; new policies (home-visit safety protocol, per-profession verification SOP).
