## What Kiorex is (use only these facts)
Kiorex is the patient-facing healthcare marketplace and network. One place where a patient can find and book doctors and clinics, order from pharmacies, and book lab tests, and keep their care in one place. Egypt is the first market; the platform is built country by country (Egypt, then Gulf countries and beyond), in Arabic and English.
Key points to explain, in this order:
1. **One identity: Kiorex Passport.** One secure sign-in with your mobile number, shared across Kiorex and the Kiorly provider products. No new password for every service.
2. **Find and book care.** Search doctors and clinics, see available times, book in a few taps; labs and pharmacies in the same app.
3. **Connected providers.** Clinics, pharmacies and labs that use Kiorly can connect to Kiorex; connected providers appear in the patient's own country market only.
4. **Your records and appointments in one place** (appointments, results, prescriptions where the provider has connected).
5. **Arabic and English**, right-to-left done properly, on web and mobile.
6. **Built for trust:** privacy by design, patients' data stays under each country's rules, verified providers.
7. **For providers:** Kiorex brings patients and tools to grow, and works together with Kiorly (the clinic, pharmacy and lab software). Mention this in one sentence only.
Closing call to action: "Kiorex. Care, connected." / Arabic: "كيوريكس. رعايتك في مكان واحد." with the website placeholder kiorex.com.

## Hard guardrails (do not break)
- Do NOT show or state any price, fee, commission rate, discount or plan. Do NOT promise live online payments, insurance coverage, medical advice, diagnosis, delivery times or any regulatory approval/licence. Do not say "AI doctor". No fake testimonials, no real patient photos or names, no real doctor names, no statistics or numbers of users/providers.
- Use only synthetic, generic characters (illustrated or stock-style, diverse, modest, Egyptian-appropriate). No real clinics, brands or logos other than Kiorex/Kiorly.
- Medical imagery must be calm and friendly (no graphic content).
- The product is in testing/pilot: add a small end card line "Launching country by country" / "نطلق دولة بعد دولة" instead of any claim of being fully live everywhere.

## Voice and music direction
- English voice: warm, calm, confident, mid-paced, friendly female or male (producer's choice, keep it consistent across Kiorex and Kiorly videos if possible). Egyptian Arabic voice: natural Cairene accent, friendly, not newsreader; pronounce "كيوريكس" (Kiorex) and "كيورلي" (Kiorly) consistently; record numbers and the website clearly.
- Music: royalty-free (document licence), light modern corporate-healthcare feel, 95 to 105 BPM, soft piano/pluck/airy pads, no vocals, a gentle build into the end card; duck music 12 to 15 dB under the voice, fade out in the last 2 seconds. Loudness target -16 LUFS stereo (-14 LUFS for the vertical cut). No copyrighted tracks.

- Accessibility: contrast AA, captions on, no flashing, readable at phone size.
