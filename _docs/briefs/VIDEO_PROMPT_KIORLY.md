## What Kiorly is (use only these facts)
Kiorly is the set of software products for care providers, by Kiorex: **Kiorly Clinic** (clinic management), **Kiorly Pharmacy**, **Kiorly Lab**, and the **Kiorly staff mobile app**. Audience: clinic owners and doctors (independent doctors, clinics, chains, hospitals), nurses and physicians, pharmacists, lab owners and technologists.
Key points to explain, in this order:
1. **One platform for providers:** run a clinic, a pharmacy or a lab calmly in one place. Each module works **standalone**, or **connected to Kiorex** to reach patients.
2. **Kiorly Clinic:** appointments and schedules, patient files, visits, billing, staff and branches (several clinics under one organisation), reports.
3. **Kiorly Pharmacy:** prescriptions queue, dispensing, stock and expiry, orders from patients when connected.
4. **Kiorly Lab:** orders, specimens and worklists, entering and releasing results, critical values.
5. **One sign-in:** Kiorex Passport (mobile number) or e-mail and password, and one place to switch between products (Kiorly Home). Staff access is by role, and security options such as a second factor can be required by the organisation.
6. **Staff mobile app:** the day-to-day work from the phone for clinic, pharmacy and lab staff.
7. **Connect to Kiorex when you want:** connected providers receive bookings and orders from Kiorex patients in their own country. A provider belongs to its country; the product follows each country's rules.
8. **Arabic and English**, right-to-left done properly. **Join by request:** providers leave a join request on the Kiorly website and the team guides the onboarding.
Closing call to action: "Kiorly. Run your care calmly." / Arabic: "كيورلي. شغّل عيادتك أو صيدليتك أو معملك بهدوء." with kiorly.com.
