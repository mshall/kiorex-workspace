## 01 Positioning
Two audiences, two promises, one proof point.

### Kiorly — for providers (B2B)
- Promise: run your clinic from your phone, in Arabic, free to start — and pay nothing extra when Kiorex fills your empty slots.
- Proof: pay the higher of subscription or commission, never both. No charge for no-shows. Your own booking link is commission-free.
- Tagline test: 'Kiorly — your clinic, organised. Free while Kiorex keeps you busy.'

### Kiorex — for patients (B2C)
- Promise: book a verified doctor with a real slot in under two minutes, and know the fee upfront.
- Proof: only verified doctors; reviews only from completed visits; instant confirmation from the clinic's own system.
- Tagline test: 'Kiorex — real doctors, real slots, real reviews.'

## 03 Beachhead: density beats coverage
A marketplace is liquid neighbourhood by neighbourhood. We launch in two Cairo/Giza micro-clusters and only expand when liquidity thresholds are met.

| Cluster (candidates) | Why |
|---|---|
| Nasr City + Heliopolis | Highest lab and clinic density in Cairo (POI data: Nasr City and Heliopolis lead lab counts) |
| New Cairo (incl. Fifth Settlement) | Young families, high smartphone use, new clinics opening |
| Mohandessin + Dokki (Giza) | Dense specialist streets |
| Maadi, 6th of October, Alexandria | Wave 2 after M9 |

Liquidity threshold to open the next cluster:
- >=150 LIVE doctors across top 8 specialties
- Median next-available slot <=48 h
- Search -> booking conversion >=8%
- >=12 attended Kiorex patients / listed doctor / month

Cluster playbook (six steps): 01 Recruit anchors (30 respected clinics) -> 02 Fill specialties (top 8 covered) -> 03 Drive demand (local SEO · Maps · social) -> 04 Measure liquidity (slots · conversion) -> 05 Open Kiorex closed beta (M4) -> 06 Next cluster (repeat).

## 10 Brand voice and messaging rules
- Arabic first, Egyptian dialect for patient copy, Modern Standard Arabic for clinical/legal; English second.
- Plain promises we can prove; no 'AI' claims beyond the AI03 registry; no superlatives about clinical outcomes.
- Doctors are heroes, not inventory: never rank by payment without a label; never shame low ratings publicly.
- Every campaign approved by the CMO for clinical accuracy.
